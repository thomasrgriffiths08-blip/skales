      04Anatomy

      What you’re
      actually paying for.

      1. Face

        3K, 12K, 18K carbon

        The K is thousands of filaments per carbon bundle. Lower tends to feel softer and more connected; higher is stiffer and hands you more pace. Fibreglass is softer still, and kinder for a first racket.

      2. Core

        Hard EVA, soft EVA, MultiEVA

        The foam inside is the engine. Hard EVA returns pace when you swing big; soft EVA does the work for you on slow balls and is easier on the elbow. MultiEVA tries to do both.

      3. Balance

        Head-heavy or even

        Weight towards the head adds power on the smash and costs you speed at the net. Even balance is quicker in your hands and forgives a late swing.

      4. Shape

        Round, teardrop, diamond

        Shape moves the sweet spot. Round keeps it low and central for control, diamond pushes it high for power, and teardrop sits in between.
