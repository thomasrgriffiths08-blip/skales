Do you need a website if you have a Facebook page?

A Facebook page is free, quick to set up and good for showing your work to people who already follow you, and for some businesses that is enough for now. But it runs on Meta’s platform and rules, it is not built to be found by people searching Google for your service, and every enquiry becomes a conversation in messages, so most businesses that need new customers end up wanting both.

When a Facebook page is the better choice

  • You are just starting and need somewhere to show your work today.
  • Nearly all your work comes from people who already know you or follow you.
  • You post often and your customers are happy to message you there.

When Skales is the better choice

  • You want to be found by people who have never heard of you.
  • You want enquiries to turn into bookings without a back-and-forth in messages.
  • You run ads, or plan to, and want the click to land somewhere that books.

Questions people ask

Is a Facebook page enough for a small business?

It can be at the start, or for a business that lives on word of mouth. Once you want new customers from search, bookings without the back-and-forth, or ads that pay their way, you need a website as well.

Should I keep my Facebook page if I get a website?

Yes. Keep posting your work there and put the website link at the top. The page keeps your followers up to date; the website is where new customers find you, read about you and book.

Do Facebook ads work better with a website?

Ads can send people to your page or to your website. Sending them to a page built for the job, with booking on it, lets you see which ads turned into booked work, which is how you know whether they are worth what you spend.
