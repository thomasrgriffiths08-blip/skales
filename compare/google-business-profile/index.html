Do you need a website if you have a Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile is free, and every local service business should have one, because it is what Google shows on the map and in local results. On its own it can be enough for a business that lives on calls from people nearby, but a website adds what the profile cannot: a page for each service and area, online booking and deposits, and a place that is yours rather than Google’s.

When a Google Business Profile alone is the better choice

  • Nearly all your work is calls from people nearby who find you on the map.
  • You offer one or two services and there is little to explain.
  • You are just starting and want to be findable today at no cost.

When Skales is

  • You offer several services, or cover several towns, and want to be found for each.
  • You want customers to book and pay a deposit without ringing you.
  • You run ads, or plan to, and need a page that turns the click into a booking.

Questions people ask

Is a Google Business Profile enough for a tradesperson?

For some, yes, especially when the work is local and arrives by phone. Once you want to be found for each service and area, take bookings online or run ads, you need pages of your own for the profile and the ads to point to.

Does a website help my Google Business Profile?

Yes. The profile links to your website, and a site whose business name, area and phone number match the profile, with a page for each service, gives Google more to go on when it decides which businesses to show.

Can I have a Google Business Profile without a shop?

Yes. Google lets businesses that visit their customers set a service area and hide their address, so a sole trader working from home can still appear in local results for the areas they cover.
