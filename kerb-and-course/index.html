+Calculators · running off your drawing

Check it before you spend

Materials, the true cost over 20 years, and the drainage and dropped-kerb rules, all from the same drawing as your price.

    Materials

    Everything that goes into your drive: dig-out, stone, sand, surface, edging and skips.

    Quantities include 5–10% for cuts and waste. MOT Type 1 compacts by about a fifth, so we order extra. Skips are 8-yard, about 10 tonnes of spoil each.

    20-year cost

    The cheapest to lay isn't always the cheapest to own. Laying cost plus 20 years of upkeep, for your size.
