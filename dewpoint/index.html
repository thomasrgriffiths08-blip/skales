    Questions

    Straight answers

    Will it take out every scratch?

    Swirls and light marks, yes. A scratch you can catch with a fingernail has gone through the clear coat. We can make it much less visible, but only paint fixes it properly. We'll show you which is which under the light before we start.

    How long does a ceramic coating last?

    The coating we use is rated for about three years. Wash it every couple of weeks with a pH-neutral shampoo and it keeps beading. We check it for free at six months.

    Do you need my water or electricity?

    No. The van carries its own filtered water and a quiet generator, so we can work on a street or in a car park.

    Can I stay in the car's house while you work?

    Of course, or go out. We only need the keys and room to walk round the car. We text you when it's done.

    What if it rains?

    Washes go ahead under a gazebo. Polishing and coating need dry paint and some cover, so we'll check the forecast with you the day before, or use your garage if you have one.
