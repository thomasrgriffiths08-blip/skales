Missed-call text-back: every missed call gets a reply.

Missed-call text-back sends a text to anyone whose call you could not answer, seconds after it rings out, with a link to book or a quick question. It turns a missed call into a conversation before they ring the next business on Google.

Why it mattersMissed-call text-back

Why does it matter?

When you are up a ladder or under a sink, you cannot answer. Plenty of people who reach voicemail do not leave a message; they ring the next number instead. A text back in the first minute keeps them talking to you.

How it worksStep by step

How does missed-call text-back work?

  1. The call rings out. You are on a job and cannot pick up. The call goes unanswered, as it would now.
  2. The text goes out. Seconds later, the caller gets a text from your business: sorry you missed them, here is a link to book, or reply and you will ring back.
  3. They book or reply. They pick a slot on your booking page, or reply with what they need. Either way they have stopped looking elsewhere.
  4. You see it all. The missed call, the text and what happened next sit on one card in your CRM, so you know who to ring and who has already booked.

What does the text say?

Sorry I missed your call, I’m on a job. You can book a time here: larchfield.co.uk/book, or reply with what you need and I’ll ring you back. Larchfield Heating

What it needsFrom you

What does it need from me?

  • A business number that can forward unanswered calls. Most UK mobile and landline networks can.
  • Your booking link, if you take bookings. If not, the text asks what they need.
  • The numbers you do not want texted, such as suppliers and family.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Missed-call text-back: questions people ask

Does it work with my mobile number?

Yes. Your phone forwards calls you do not answer, and the text goes out from there. You keep your number and answer calls exactly as you do now.

Will it text suppliers, family or spam callers?

Numbers you know can be left out, so only new callers get the text.

How fast does the text go?

Within seconds of the call ringing out, while the caller still has their phone in their hand.

Will it spam my customers?

No. It only texts people who have just rung you, once, about the call they made. Nobody is added to a marketing list.
