Booking deposits: a booked slot that turns into a job.

A booking deposit is a small payment the customer makes when they book online, which holds the slot and comes off the final bill. It turns a booking into a commitment, so the time you set aside is paid for.

Book a callTry the demo
Why it mattersBooking deposits

Why does it matter?

A no-show costs you the job and the job you turned away to fit them in. A deposit is the simplest fix: a booking with money behind it is a booking people keep, and the ones who cannot make it let you know.

How it worksStep by step

How does booking deposits work?

  1. They pick a slot. The customer chooses the job and a free time on your booking page.
  2. They pay the deposit. They pay by card on the same page. You choose which jobs take a deposit and how much.
  3. The slot is theirs. They get a confirmation with the deposit shown, and a reminder before the day.
  4. It comes off the bill. The deposit is taken off the final invoice. Your cancellation rules decide what happens if they cancel late.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Booking deposits: questions people ask

Will a deposit put customers off?

A small deposit on the booking page is normal for salons, clinics, restaurants and more and more trades. Customers who are serious about the job pay it; the ones who were never going to turn up tend not to book.

Where does the money go?

Straight to your own payment account, set up in your name.

What if a customer cancels?

Your rules decide. Many businesses refund with enough notice and keep the deposit for late cancellations, and the booking page says so before they pay.

Can some jobs be booked without a deposit?

Yes. You choose which jobs take one, so a free quote visit can be booked without paying anything.
