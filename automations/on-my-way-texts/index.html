On-my-way texts: the customer knows you are coming.

An on-my-way text tells the customer you have set off, with a rough arrival time and who is coming, so they are in, the dog is shut away and the car is off the drive. One tap from the job sheet sends it.

Why it mattersOn-my-way texts

Why does it matter?

A wasted trip to an empty house costs a morning. A customer who is not sure when you are coming rings to ask, and calls you while you are driving. One text before you set off fixes both.

How it worksStep by step

How does on-my-way texts work?

  1. You set off. On the way to the job, you tap On my way on the job sheet on your phone.
  2. The text goes out. The customer gets a text with who is coming and a rough arrival time.
  3. They get ready. They are in, the parking is clear, and they are not ringing you to ask where you are.
  4. Running late is one tap too. If a job overruns, one tap tells the next customer, so nobody is left waiting without knowing why.
The messageWhat the customer gets

What does the text say?

Hi Sam, Dan from Larchfield Heating is on his way and should be with you around 9:40. Reply if anything’s changed.

An example for Larchfield Heating, a made-up business. Yours is written with you, in your words.

  • A job sheet on your phone, which the CRM gives you.
  • Who is on which job, if more than one of you goes out.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

On-my-way texts: questions people ask

Does it track my location?

No. It sends the text when you tap the button, with the arrival time you choose. Nobody is followed on a map.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Each person taps from their own job sheet, and the text says who is coming.

What about the night before?

Every booking already gets a reminder the day before. The on-my-way text is for the morning of the job.

Is this only for trades?

No. It suits anyone who goes to the customer: cleaners, mobile hairdressers, dog groomers, mechanics, surveyors.
