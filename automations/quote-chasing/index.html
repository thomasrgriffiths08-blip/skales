Quote chasing: no quote goes quiet without a follow-up.

Quote chasing sends a friendly follow-up when a quote has not been accepted, a couple of days after it went out, with a link to accept or ask a question. Quotes that go quiet get a nudge without you having to remember who you priced up last Tuesday.

Why it mattersQuote chasing

Why does it matter?

Pricing a job takes time, and most quotes are never chased because the person who wrote them is on another job. A customer who has gone quiet is often just busy. The one who follows up politely is the one they book.

How it worksStep by step

How does quote chasing work?

  1. The quote goes out. You price the job and send it from the CRM. The customer gets a link to see it and accept it.
  2. The system watches. It knows whether they have opened it, and whether they have accepted.
  3. The chaser goes out. If the quote is still open after a couple of days, the customer gets a short, friendly follow-up with the link again.
  4. Accepted becomes booked. When they accept, the job moves to Booked and the confirmation goes out. If they say no, it is closed and you know.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Quote chasing: questions people ask

Will customers find it pushy?

Not one polite message a couple of days later. It reads like a business that cares about the job, and customers often reply to say they had simply not got round to it.

How many times does it chase?

Once by default, with a second follow-up if you want one. After that the quote is left alone.

What if the customer has already accepted?

Then no chaser goes. It only follows up quotes that are still open.

Can I still send my own quotes?

Yes. You price the job; the system sends it and follows it up.
