Service reminders: the work that comes round, booked on its own.

Service reminders text your customers when they are due again: the annual boiler service, the MOT, the next haircut, the gutter clean. The work that comes round every year gets booked without anyone having to remember to ring.

Why it mattersService reminders

Why does it matter?

Repeat work is the cheapest work you will ever win, and it is the easiest to lose. Customers do not forget you; they forget the date. Whoever reminds them first gets the job.

How it worksStep by step

How does service reminders work?

  1. The job sets the date. When a job that comes round is done (a service, an MOT, a cut), the next due date is worked out and saved with the customer.
  2. The reminder goes out. A few weeks before they are due, the customer gets a text saying so, with a link to book.
  3. A nudge if needed. If they do not book, one follow-up goes out closer to the date. Then it stops.
  4. The diary fills. Bookings from reminders land in your diary like any other, marked as returning customers.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Service reminders: questions people ask

Which businesses is this for?

Any business where the work comes round: boiler services and landlord certificates, MOTs and car services, hair and beauty appointments, window and gutter cleaning, check-ups and maintenance contracts.

Can it use my existing customer list?

Yes. A spreadsheet with names, numbers and the date of their last job is enough to start.

How many reminders does a customer get?

Usually one, and one follow-up if they do not book. Then it stops until next time.

Is it allowed to text past customers?

A reminder about a service they have had before, from a business they used, is the kind of message most customers expect. Anyone who replies to opt out is never texted again.
