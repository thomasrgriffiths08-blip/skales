What does Starboard do?

Starboard is a working tool built to work for any local business. Review tools mostly nag the owner and write the replies for them. Asking well and replying in your own voice is the whole job, so the tool does the timing and leaves the words alone.

It sits in the Broad lane: works for any service business: booking, quotes, the pipeline, follow-up, reviews.

What is in it

  • Twelve completed jobs seeded against the real clock, each with an ask timeline and a follow-up you can push three days
  • Ask now shows the actual message, the customer opens it, and a new review lands at the top with a subtle highlight
  • Reply box with three starters the owner edits — nothing is written for them and passed off as theirs
  • Rating over twelve weeks as a line, new reviews per week as bars, both on solid hairline grids with direct labels
