An accountant's site that counts down to your next deadline from the first screen.

A practice site in ledger navy and white with one green for anything on time. What does Hale & Partners do?

Hale & Partners is a working website built for an invented accountant. Built to prove a quiet site can still work: no photographs of handshakes, no stock office, just the two things an accountant is actually judged on: the dates and the numbers.

What is in it

  • The next deadline counted in days in the first screen, worked out from the visitor's own date
  • Seven services as a ledger: what you hand over, and what you get back
  • Three questions that light up the one line to start with
  • A sample year-end statement that settles from last year's figures to this year's, then ticks itself filed
  • A fee request that asks for a turnover band instead of publishing a price

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
No frameworks. Fonts are served from this site with system fallbacks, and the drawings, charts and interactions are written into the page.
Imagery
Real photographs from Pexels, free for commercial use, graded and cropped for this page. Nobody pictured is presented as staff. Everything else is drawn in code.
The business
Chartered accountants, Harrogate is invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser, on a phone as much as a desktop.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
