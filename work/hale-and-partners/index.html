Hale & Partners

A year-end that settles, and the deadlines counted from today.

A practice site set out as a well-kept ledger: services written as double entries — what you hand over on the left, what you get back on the right — and a real set of accounts with last year beside this year. The figures count to their values as the statement comes into view and a green tick draws itself beside Filed; the deadline calendar is worked out from the day the visitor opens the page, so it is never out of date.

What does Hale & Partners do?

Hale & Partners is a working website built for an invented accountant. Built to prove a quiet site can still work: no photographs of handshakes, no stock office, just the two things an accountant is actually judged on — the dates and the numbers.

It sits in the Quiet lane: plain, calm, expensive. almost nothing moves; the typography does the work.

What is in it

  • A year-end statement whose column settles as it enters view, tick and all
  • Services as Dr and Cr lines: what you hand over, what you get back
  • UK deadlines with the days remaining, computed from today's date
  • A three-question triage that points at one service line and stops
  • A fee request that asks for a band instead of publishing a price
