What does Redgate Job & Lead System do?

Redgate Job & Lead System is a working tool built for an invented heating & plumbing. The one every service business actually needs and almost none has: a single place where every enquiry lives with what happened to it. Built for a heating firm; the same bones fit any trade.

It sits in the Broad lane: works for any service business: booking, quotes, the pipeline, follow-up, reviews.

What is in it

  • Kanban board and sortable table over the same jobs
  • Deal drawer with a full activity timeline
  • Cmd-K command palette across deals, contacts and actions
  • Channels, calls, reviews and money as first-class views
  • CSV export, filters and saved views

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
None at runtime. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; everything else — every drawing, chart and interaction — is in the file.
Imagery
No stock. Every visual is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.
The business
Heating & plumbing — invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser. It is designed for a desktop or tablet; on a phone, open it full screen. State stays in your browser; there is a reset control inside.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
