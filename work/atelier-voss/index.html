What does Atelier Voss do?

Atelier Voss is a working website built for an invented architecture practice. An architect sells the drawing and the building. This site shows both, side by side, and links them point by point.

What is in it

  • The name huge over a full-screen photo of a finished building
  • Four projects, each a photo beside its drawing sheet
  • Plans, sections and elevations that draw themselves as you scroll
  • Numbered callouts linking each drawing to the same spot in the photo
  • An enquiry form laid out as the practice's own title block

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
No frameworks. Fonts are served from this site with system fallbacks, and the drawings, charts and interactions are written into the page.
Imagery
Real photographs from Pexels, free for commercial use, graded and cropped for this page. Nobody pictured is presented as staff. Everything else is drawn in code.
The business
Architecture practice is invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser, on a phone as much as a desktop.
