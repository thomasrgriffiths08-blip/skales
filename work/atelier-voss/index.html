What does Atelier Voss do?

Atelier Voss is a working website built for an invented architecture practice. Proof that a site can be beautiful with nothing bought in — every line on it was drawn for it.

It sits in the Quiet lane: plain, calm, expensive. almost nothing moves; the typography does the work.

What is in it

  • Every image is a hand-authored SVG drawing
  • Elevations that draw themselves in on scroll
  • A real title block as the masthead
  • No photography, no stock, no templates

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
None at runtime. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; everything else — every drawing, chart and interaction — is in the file.
Imagery
No stock. Every visual is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.
The business
Architecture practice — invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser. It is designed for a desktop or tablet; on a phone, open it full screen. State stays in your browser; there is a reset control inside.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
