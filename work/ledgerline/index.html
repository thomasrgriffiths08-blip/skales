What does Ledgerline do?

Ledgerline is a working tool built to work for any trade. Invoices are the last thing a tradesperson does on a Sunday night. This makes one in the time it takes to read the job sheet.

It sits in the Broad lane: works for any service business: booking, quotes, the pipeline, follow-up, reviews.

What is in it

  • Live A4 preview that reflows as you type
  • Three genuinely different templates
  • Exact money maths in integer pence
  • Aged-debt view and print-to-PDF
