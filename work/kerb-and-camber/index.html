Kerb & Camber

A UK number plate you type into, and the MOT date that comes back.

thomasrgriffiths08-blip.github.io/skales/demos/kerb-and-camber/

An independent MOT and service centre where the hero is a real number plate you can type in. Press Check and a job card comes back with the vehicle, the last advisory, how many days until the MOT runs out, and the next three slots on the ramp. Below it sit the price board from the office door, each line with its own Book button, the clock times of a real MOT from keys to away, and a half-hour diary. On a phone the call and book buttons never leave the screen.

What does Kerb & Camber do?

Kerb & Camber is a working website built for an invented garage. Half the calls a garage takes are somebody asking when their MOT is due. Answering it on the page turns that call into a booking.

What is in it

  • Number-plate input with a fictional lookup that is deterministic and labelled as a demo
  • MOT due or overdue counted against the real clock, with the next three free slots on the ramp
  • Price board as it hangs by the office door, with a Book button on each line that picks the job for you
  • Half-hour slot booking on a diary built from today, Sundays closed, ending in a booking reference
  • Real workshop photos, opening hours that say open now or opens at, and a call and book bar on phones

Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.

No frameworks. Fonts are served from this site with system fallbacks, and the drawings, charts and interactions are written into the page.
Imagery
Real photographs by Artem Podrez on Pexels, free for commercial use. Everything else is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.
