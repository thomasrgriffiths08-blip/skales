Kerb & Camber

Type your reg into a real number plate and the MOT date and next free slots come back.

skales.studio/demos/kerb-and-camber/

The hero is a UK number plate set into the workshop photo: type the reg, press Check, and the headline turns into the answer, "MOT due in 247 days.", with the next three free half-hours under it. Tap one and the booking sheet is already filled in: MOT, the day, the time and the reg. Below it a pinned job card walks a real MOT from 08:30 to 09:40, then the price board as it hangs by the office door.

What it does for the owner

Somebody who does not know when their MOT is due can find out and book it before they close the tab.

Half the calls a garage takes are somebody asking when their MOT is due. Answering it on the page turns that call into a booking.

  1. The reg goes into a real number plate and the headline answers with the MOT date, so nobody has to dig out the V5
  2. The next three free half-hours sit under the answer, and one tap fills in the booking with the day, the time and the reg
  3. A pinned job card walks a real MOT from 08:30 to 09:40, so a first-time customer knows exactly what happens
  4. Every line on the price board has its own Book button, so the price and the booking are one tap apart
