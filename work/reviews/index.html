Google reviews

Every finished job asks for a review. Replies take one tap.

After each job the customer gets a short text with a link to leave a Google review. Every customer is asked, not only the happy ones, because Google’s rules ban picking. New reviews land here with a reply already written to edit and post.

What is this part for?

The firms at the top of the map are rarely the best; they are the ones who ask. This asks every time.

What is in it

  • A review ask after every finished job

What else is in the system?

Every part shares the same customers, diary and messages, so a missed call becomes a booking, the booking becomes a review ask, and the review shows up in the monthly results without anyone typing it twice.
