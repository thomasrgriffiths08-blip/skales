What does Southpaw do?

Southpaw is a working website built for an invented boxing club. Gyms sell on atmosphere and then hide the timetable three clicks deep. This one puts the noise and the booking in the same place, so the page feels like the gym and still books the session.

What is in it

  • A working round timer in the first screen: three-minute rounds, a minute's rest, twelve rounds, with the digits turning red for the last ten seconds
  • The week's timetable straight underneath, worked out from the visitor's clock: what's on now, the next bell, and what has already finished
  • First-session booking that starts with four photo tiles (never boxed, done a bit, competed, it's for my kid), then a day this week, then two fields
  • Real gym photography, membership with a monthly or annual switch, and a book and call bar that stays on screen on phones

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
No frameworks. Fonts are served from this site with system fallbacks, and the drawings, charts and interactions are written into the page.
Imagery
Real photographs from Pexels, free for commercial use, graded and cropped for this page. Nobody pictured is presented as staff. Everything else is drawn in code.
The business
Boxing club, Leeds is invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser, on a phone as much as a desktop.
