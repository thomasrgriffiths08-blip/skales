What does Doorstep do?

Doorstep is a working tool built to work for any service business. Customers ring because they cannot see anything. Giving them one link that answers where, who, what and how much turns the anxious afternoon phone call into a page they check once.

It sits in the Broad lane: works for any service business: booking, quotes, the pipeline, follow-up, reviews.

What is in it

  • Seven-step job timeline from booked to paid, with the engineer, his van reg and a live ETA that counts down while he is on the way
  • Business view with status buttons that drive the customer page in real time — the route line and countdown start the moment it is pressed
  • Quote, invoice and certificate as document cards, each with a download that goes to a saved state and stays saved
  • Demo card payment against the balance with the deposit already taken, and a message thread the office answers

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in

None at runtime. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; everything else — every drawing, chart and interaction — is in the file.

No stock. Every visual is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.

Runs on
