What does Mortgage Lab do?

Mortgage Lab is a working tool built for an invented mortgage broker. A lead magnet that is actually useful. People come back to it — and a broker is the name on it when they do.

It sits in the Specific lane: built around one trade’s actual day: its diary, its paperwork, its reminders.

What is in it

  • Month-by-month amortisation engine
  • Overpayment and lump-sum modelling
  • Remortgage stress test at +1, +2 and +3 points
  • Three saved scenarios compared side by side

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
None at runtime. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; everything else — every drawing, chart and interaction — is in the file.
Imagery
No stock. Every visual is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.
The business
Broker lead magnet — invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser. It is designed for a desktop or tablet; on a phone, open it full screen. State stays in your browser; there is a reset control inside.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
