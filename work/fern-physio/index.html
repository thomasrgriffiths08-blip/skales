What it does for the owner

A visitor can say where it hurts before they have to say anything to a receptionist.

Clinics lose people at the phone call, because a sore shoulder is hard to describe to a stranger. Pointing at a drawing is not.

  1. Patients tap where it hurts on a drawn body, so they never have to explain it to a receptionist
  2. The next three free slots sit beside the answer, so pointing at a knee becomes a booking in one more tap
  3. The first visit is told minute by minute, which takes away the nerves that stop people booking
  4. The first visit and its price, £62, are on the first screen, and a booking bar stays in reach on a phone
