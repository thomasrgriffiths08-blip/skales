What it does for the owner

Software can be sold by letting people use it on the first screen.

Route and shift software for cold-chain field crews. The board is not a screenshot: scroll and a shift plays out, a job slips behind a crash on the A629, and you decide whether to move the crew.

  1. The product is the first screen, so a buyer sees what it does before reading a word
  2. At 07:40 a job slips and the board prices it, so the value shows up in pounds rather than adjectives
  3. The visitor takes, keeps or undoes the crew swap, so they make the coordinator’s call themselves
  4. Pricing laid out as a monthly statement that does the maths, and a walkthrough booking that remembers the slot
