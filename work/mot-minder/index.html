What does MOT Minder do?

MOT Minder is a working tool built for an invented garage. Garages already know which cars are due — the list is sat in the diary. What they lose is the twenty minutes a day it takes to chase them, so the car goes to whoever texted first.

It sits in the Specific lane: built around one trade’s actual day: its diary, its paperwork, its reminders.

What is in it

  • Forty vehicles with plate chips, MOT and service dates counted from today, sorted, filtered and searchable
  • Send the rebook link and watch the customer take a slot — the booking lands in the grid and the row flips
  • Advisories from the last MOT tick into a quote with VAT and go out by text
  • The six-week, two-week and three-day wording, with merge fields and the text as the customer reads it
  • Reminded versus rebooked by month, and which advisories actually turned into work
  • Everything survives a reload; Reset demo puts the forty cars back
