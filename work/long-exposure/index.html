What does Long Exposure do?

Long Exposure is a working website built for an invented film production company. Film companies tend to bury the reel behind a play button on a black page. This one opens on it, keeps the timecode honest, and lets you scrub straight to the job you care about.

It sits in the Motion lane: the page is the film: a showreel first screen, scroll-driven sequences, timecodes.

What is in it

  • A ten second showreel cut from four real clips fills the first screen, with a phone-sized portrait cut for phones
  • A running timecode and a scrub bar sit on the footage, one segment per job: drag, tap a job or use the arrow keys
  • Four jobs that play on hover on a laptop and as they come into view on a phone, each one able to jump the reel to its shot
  • Services set out as a real call sheet, dated next Tuesday with Manchester's sunrise and sunset worked out for that day
  • Add a line from the sheet and it lands on the request, which comes back as a call sheet with who rings you and when
