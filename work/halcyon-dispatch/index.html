What does Halcyon Dispatch do?

Halcyon Dispatch is a working tool built for an invented heating dispatch. The morning phone round — "where are you, can you take one more" — replaced by a board that already knows.

It sits in the Broad lane: works for any service business: booking, quotes, the pipeline, follow-up, reviews.

What is in it

  • Week and day views for a whole team
  • Drag-and-drop rescheduling with clash and capacity warnings
  • SLA countdowns and an emergency queue
  • Statuses derived from the real clock, so the board is never stale
