What it does for the owner

A survey firm can show what it hands over, not just a gallery of aerial photos.

Drone operators all show the same thing: pretty aerial photographs. What they are paid for is measurement, so the page draws the survey over the photo, the way the client will see it.

  1. The first screen flies straight down with live telemetry, so it reads as surveyors, not hobby pilots
  2. Each service draws itself over an aerial photo as you scroll, showing exactly what the client gets handed
  3. The price sits beside every survey, so the call starts with the job rather than the cost
  4. A quote form that starts with the postcode and gives back a job reference and a flight log
