What does Payslip Lab do?

Payslip Lab is a working tool built for an invented accountant. Every accountant answers the same first question. This answers it before the call, and the call is then about the interesting part.

It sits in the Specific lane: built around one trade’s actual day: its diary, its paperwork, its reminders.

What is in it

  • Gross-to-net waterfall for the current tax year
  • Marginal-rate curve showing the personal-allowance taper
  • Employee vs sole trader vs limited company comparison
  • Reverse solver: “what gross do I need for this net?”
