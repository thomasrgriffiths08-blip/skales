Kestrel Electrical

An electrician's page built as a fuse board — every job is a breaker. Three lines say what they do, where they go and when they answer; under them sit three fields and the phone number at poster size. The services are a row of breakers on a board — flip one and it turns on to show what's included, what it costs and a button to ring about it. The only thing that moves on the page is a switch.

What does Kestrel Electrical do?

Kestrel Electrical is a working website built for an invented electrician. Most trade sites bury the number under a hero image and a form nobody fills in. Borrowing the layout of the thing an electrician opens every day made the page label itself: everything is a switch, and the biggest one rings the phone.

It sits in the Direct lane: conversion first. one-tap call, a three-field form, the service area, no fluff.

What is in it

  • Seven breakers on a consumer unit — fault finding, fuse board, EV charger, rewire, EICR, sockets, emergency — each flipping on to its own panel
  • The main switch is the phone number, and it swaps between the office line and the emergency mobile from the real clock
  • Callback form that validates and confirms by name, number and the hour the office will ring
  • Postcode outward-code check across NG1–NG17, and a sticky CALL / BOOK bar on phones
