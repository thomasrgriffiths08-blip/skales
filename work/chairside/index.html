What does Chairside do?

Chairside is a working tool built for an invented hair salon. Salons lose more money to empty chairs than to bad haircuts. The two moments that fix it are the thirty seconds at the counter and the ten minutes after a cancellation, so the tool is built around exactly those two.

It sits in the Specific lane: built around one trade’s actual day: its diary, its paperwork, its reminders.

What is in it

  • Today's diary across four stylists on a real time grid, seeded for the whole week and read against the clock
  • Checkout as the client sees it on the counter tablet: three suggested slots, add-on, tip and a demo card, then a rebooked badge in the diary
  • No-show shield — the clients with history, the card-holding rule, and a 48h/24h reminder sequence with reply-C-to-cancel
  • Cancel an appointment and the waitlist is texted, the first yes takes the slot, and the diary refills in front of you
