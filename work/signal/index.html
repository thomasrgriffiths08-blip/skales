What it does for the owner

Bold, and still easy to use.

The bold one, rebuilt around the crowd. Everything on it helps someone decide to go: who is on, when, where, and what it costs.

  1. The name, the dates and the ticket price are all on the first screen, over the stage film
  2. The line-up is a printed poster you tap to build your weekend, and it stamps any clash
  3. The slip underneath draws each night hour by hour with the overlap marked, so nobody misses the act they came for
  4. Ticket tiers show live availability and the booking fee before you hold, the thing that loses festival sales at checkout
