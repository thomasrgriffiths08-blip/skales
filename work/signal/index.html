What does SIGNAL 2027 do?

SIGNAL 2027 is a working website built for an invented festival. The loud one, rebuilt around the crowd. Everything on it helps someone decide to go: who is on, when, where, and what it costs.

What is in it

  • Real crowd, stage and venue photography with a live countdown to doors
  • The line-up as a still poster sized by billing: tap a name for its day, room and time
  • Build your weekend from the poster, with clashes flagged as you go
  • Four ticket tiers with live totals, the booking fee shown up front and a 15 minute hold

How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
No frameworks. Fonts are served from this site with system fallbacks, and the drawings, charts and interactions are written into the page.
Imagery
Real photographs from Pexels, free for commercial use, graded and cropped for this page. Nobody pictured is presented as staff. Everything else is drawn in code.
The business
Music & light festival is invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser, on a phone as much as a desktop.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
