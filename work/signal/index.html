SIGNAL 2027

Type that reacts to how hard you scroll. Three days of sound, code and light. Marquees whose speed and skew answer your scrolling, headlines that scramble into place, an RGB fringe on the display type because the identity is light being split, and a live countdown in enormous tabular numerals.

Powering up

This is the build itself, not a recording. Use it like a customer would. State is kept in your browser; there is a reset inside.

What it provesLoud, and still not a template.

What does SIGNAL 2027 do?

SIGNAL 2027 is a working website built for an invented music & light festival. The loud one. Every effect on it is tied to the subject — light being split — rather than bolted on.

What is in it

  • Marquees that answer scroll velocity
  • Headlines that scramble into place
  • RGB fringe on display type
  • Live countdown, schedule grid, ticket maths
Scroll-velocity typeRGB fringeLive countdown
How it was builtBy hand, with nothing bought in
Written
By hand, as a single self-contained page. No page-builder, no theme, no framework.
Dependencies
None at runtime. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; everything else — every drawing, chart and interaction — is in the file.
Imagery
No stock. Every visual is drawn in code, SVG or canvas.
The business
Music & light festival — invented. Every person, number and review inside is fictional so nothing here exposes a real client.
Runs on
Any modern browser, phone included. State stays in your browser; there is a reset control inside.
Yours would
Carry your name, your numbers and your diary, and be owned by you from day one.
