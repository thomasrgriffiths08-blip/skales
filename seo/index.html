Why it works

  1. Search engines rank what they understand.

    A page that says plainly what the business does, where and for whom, in its title, headings and schema, is easier to match to a search than a clever slogan. Each service and each area gets its own page, so each one can answer its own search.

  2. Local searches run on trust.

    For “near me” searches Google leans on your Business Profile, your reviews and seeing the same name, address and number everywhere. The site repeats those exact details in its schema, so the two agree.

  3. AI answers quote sentences, not slogans.

    Assistants build answers from pages they can read and lift short facts that stand on their own. “Kestrel fits 7 kW chargers across Nottingham from £850” can be quoted. “Powering your tomorrow” cannot.

  4. A fast page turns a visit into a call.

    A lot of these searches happen on a phone, often in a hurry. A page that loads quickly with the number in reach gets the call that a slow one loses.

What goes into every page

Twelve things, set up by hand and checked on every build. Most of them nobody sees, which is why most sites skip them.

  • <title>Title tagThe blue line in a search result. Written for the search people type, “Electrician in Nottingham”, not “Home”.
  • <meta description>Meta descriptionThe two lines under the title. It does not lift rankings on its own, but it is the advert that earns the click.
  • <h1> <h2>HeadingsOne H1 per page and H2s shaped like the questions people ask, so the page reads as an answer.
  • application/ld+jsonSchema (JSON-LD)The business type, address, hours, services and prices, stated in schema.org’s shared vocabulary.
  • rel="canonical"Canonical addressOne true address for each page, so copies and tracking links do not split the credit.
  • og:imageShare cardsOpen Graph tags and a designed image, so a link sent on WhatsApp or Facebook shows the right picture and title.
  • alt="…"Alt textEvery photo described in words, for screen readers and for image search.
  • /sitemap.xmlSitemapA list of every page with the date it changed, so new pages are found without waiting.
  • /robots.txtRobots rulesWhat search and AI crawlers may read. Left open for the pages you want found.
  • /llms.txtllms.txtA plain summary of the business and its pages for AI tools to read first.
  • IndexNow pingIndexNowWhen a page is published or changed, Bing and the engines that use IndexNow are told straight away.
  • LCP · CLS · INPSpeed on a phoneHand-written pages, sized images and no page builder. Google measures how fast and steady a page loads.

Questions, answered

What is SEO?

Search engine optimisation: building and writing a website so search engines can read it, understand what the business does and where, and show it to people searching for that. For a local business it mostly means clear pages for each service and area, accurate details, a fast site on a phone, and a well kept Google Business Profile.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation: making a website easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews to read and quote. They build answers from pages they can fetch, and they lift short, self-contained facts, so prices, areas, hours and what is included are written as plain sentences.

What is schema markup?

Schema, or structured data, is a block of code in the page (JSON-LD) that states facts in a shared vocabulary from schema.org: this is an electrician, here is the address, these are the hours, this service starts at this price. Search engines and AI tools read it without having to guess from the design.

What is metadata?

The information about a page that sits in its code rather than on screen: the title tag and meta description that make up a search result, the canonical address, and the Open Graph tags that decide the picture and title when the link is shared.

Does schema guarantee stars or rich results in Google?

No. Schema helps search engines understand a page and can make it eligible for richer results, but Google decides what to show. Anyone promising stars from schema alone is overselling.

How long does SEO take?

New pages usually take weeks to months to settle in the results, and local rankings also depend on your Google Business Profile and reviews. Ads can bring enquiries in the meantime.

Do I still need a Google Business Profile?

Yes. For searches like “electrician near me”, the map results come from Google Business Profiles. The website backs the profile up with the same name, address, number and hours, written into its schema.

What is llms.txt?

A plain text file at the root of a site that summarises the business and lists its key pages for AI tools. It is a proposed standard: some tools read it and some do not yet, and it costs nothing to have. This site has one.
