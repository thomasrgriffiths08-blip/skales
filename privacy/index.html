Privacy notice

Skales Studio is Tom Griffiths, one person in the United Kingdom. This page says what personal information the site and the studio collect, why, who else sees it, how long it is kept, and how to have it changed or deleted.

Who is responsible for your information?

Tom Griffiths, trading as Skales Studio, in the United Kingdom. For anything on this page, email thomasrgriffiths08@gmail.com, or message on WhatsApp.

What is collected, and why?

The three questions on /start. Your answers stay in your browser until you book a call; then they go into the booking with where you arrived from, so I can prepare.

Booking a call. The booking page asks for your name, business, town, phone, email and what you would like help with. They go to Calendly, which runs the booking calendar, and I use them to prepare for the call.

The free teardown. Your name, number and a line about your business, sent from your own WhatsApp or email.

Messages. Whatever you send me by email, WhatsApp or Instagram, so I can reply.

The live demos. Anything you type into the demos on /try/ stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Visiting the site. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, run by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser and the pages requested) to keep it running and secure. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Ads and analytics. None at the moment: the site runs no analytics or advertising tags. If that changes, a cookie banner will ask you first and this page will say so.

Clients. If we work together: your contact and business details, the accounts and content the build needs, and invoices.

On what basis?

Replying to an enquiry you started and preparing a quote: steps you asked for before a contract, and my legitimate interest in answering. Doing the work: the contract. Keeping invoices and records: the law. Server logs: my legitimate interest in keeping the site working and secure.

Who else sees it?

Nobody buys it, and it is never sold or swapped. The services that handle it for me are the site’s host (Cloudflare) and Calendly for bookings, plus email and WhatsApp when you choose to use them, and anyone the law requires me to share it with.

Does it leave the UK?

Some of those services are run by companies based in the United States, including Cloudflare, Google and Calendly. Where information goes outside the UK, it is covered by the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or the contract clauses those companies use.

How long is it kept?

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within 12 months of the last message. For clients, information is kept while we work together, and invoices and business records for as long as tax law requires.

What can you ask for?

You can ask to see what is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how it is used, get a copy to take elsewhere. Email thomasrgriffiths08@gmail.com, or message on WhatsApp, and you will get an answer within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
