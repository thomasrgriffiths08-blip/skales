    How it is set up

    Built on your business, not a script

    Before it takes a call it is given your diary, your price list and the rules you work by. That is how it can book the job, and how it knows when to stop.

    • Your diary.It offers only the times that are really free, and the booking goes straight in.
    • Your prices.It quotes from your price list. If a job is not on it, it says so and does not guess.
    • Your rules.The safety question you always ask, your deposit, your cancellation terms. It follows them on every call.
    • You, when it matters.Anything urgent or unusual goes to your phone with the details. Every call leaves you a summary.

    Straight answers

    What people ask first

    Are the calls on this page real?

    No. They are recorded examples: scripts written for made-up businesses and spoken by two AI voices, so you can hear how a call goes and what the assistant does during it. An agent for your business is set up on your own diary, price list and rules.

    Will my callers know it is an AI?

    Yes. It says so in its first sentence. Nobody is tricked into thinking they are talking to a person.

    What happens when it cannot answer something?

    It says so, takes the details, tells the caller who will ring them back, and sends you the note. It does not guess at prices or advice.

    Does it replace the person who answers my phone?

    It covers the calls nobody can take: when you are on a job, with a customer, or closed for the night. Anything it should not handle is passed to a person with the details.

    What does an AI phone agent cost?

    A flat price for the build, agreed on a call before anything starts. Book a call and you will get a straight answer for your business.

    A breakdown at 23:10 · Larchfield Heating

    Dan clocked off at 17:30. The phone rings at 23:10.

    AI assistant: Good evening, Larchfield Heating. I’m the AI assistant. Dan has finished for the day, but I can help you now. What’s happened?

    Sam: Hi. My boiler is showing an F28 fault and there’s no hot water.

    AI assistant: Sorry to hear that. One safety question first. Can you smell gas at all?

    If the caller says: No, nothing like that

    Sam: No, nothing like that.

    AI assistant: Good. Is any water leaking from the boiler?

    Sam: No, it’s dry. The pressure’s on 1.5.

    AI assistant: Thanks. That tells Dan which parts to bring. A breakdown visit starts at £95, and he’s free tomorrow morning at eight. Shall I book it?

    Sam: Yes, please. It’s Sam Whitfield, 12 Ash Grove.

    AI assistant: You’re booked, Sam. Tomorrow at eight. I’ve texted you a link for the £30 deposit, which comes off the bill, and Dan will text when he’s on his way.

    Sam: Brilliant. Thank you.

    AI assistant: You’re welcome. Good night.

    A job booked at 23:11 while Dan slept.

    If the caller says: Yes, a little

    Sam: Yes, a little, near the boiler.

    AI assistant: Then please don’t wait for us. Hang up and ring the gas emergency line now, on 0800 111 999. Don’t touch any switches, and open the windows if you can. I’m texting you that number, and I’m ringing Dan now.

    Sam: Okay. I’ll ring them now.

    AI assistant: Good. Stay safe.

    No booking. The safe answer instead.

    Moving an appointment · Fern Physio

    Both physios are with patients. Nobody is at the desk.

    AI assistant: Hello, Fern Physio. I’m the clinic’s AI assistant. The physios are both with patients, so I can help. What do you need?

    Hannah: Hi, it’s Hannah Price. I’ve got an appointment on Thursday, but I can’t make it now.

    AI assistant: No problem, Hannah. I can see you’re with Dev on Thursday at half past four. Would you like to move it, or cancel it?

    If the caller says: Move it to Friday

    Hannah: Can I move it? Friday would be better.

    AI assistant: Dev has Friday at quarter past ten, or Friday at two. Which suits you?

    Hannah: Two o’clock, please.

    AI assistant: Done. You’re with Dev on Friday at two, and Thursday’s slot is open again. I’ve texted you the new time. Anything else?

    Hannah: No, that’s great. Thanks.

    AI assistant: Thanks, Hannah. See you on Friday.

    Moved without anyone leaving the treatment room.

    If the caller says: Cancel it

    Hannah: Cancel it, please. I’ll rebook when I know my shifts.

    AI assistant: That’s cancelled, with no charge, because it’s more than a day’s notice. I’ve offered Thursday’s slot to the next person on the waiting list. Shall I text you the booking link for later?

    Hannah: Yes, please.

    AI assistant: It’s on its way. Take care, Hannah.

    Cancelled by the clinic’s own rules, and the slot offered on.

    A question it cannot answer · Kerb & Camber

    Both mechanics are under a car. The office phone rings.

    AI assistant: Good afternoon, Kerb and Camber. I’m the garage’s AI assistant. Both mechanics are under a car, so I can help. What do you need?

    Owen: Hi. My MOT runs out next week. Have you got anything before Friday?

    AI assistant: Yes. I have Wednesday at nine, or Thursday at half past one. An MOT is £54.85.

    Owen: Wednesday at nine, please. Also, there’s a knocking noise when I brake. What will that cost to fix?

    AI assistant: I can’t price that until a mechanic has looked at it, and I won’t guess. I can add a brake check to Wednesday, and ask Baz to ring you today. Would you like that?

    If the caller says: Yes, ask him to ring me

    Owen: Yes, ask him to ring me.

    AI assistant: Done. I’ve booked the MOT for Wednesday at nine, with a brake check added, and Baz has your number and a note about the noise. Can I take your name?

    Owen: It’s Owen Hughes.

    AI assistant: Thanks, Owen. A confirmation text is on its way. See you on Wednesday.

    The MOT booked, and the hard question passed to a person.

    If the caller says: Just the MOT for now

    Owen: Just the MOT for now.

    AI assistant: No problem. I’ve booked the MOT for Wednesday at nine, and put a note on the job about the noise, so the tester knows to listen for it. Can I take your name?

    Owen: It’s Owen Hughes.

    AI assistant: Thanks, Owen. A confirmation text is on its way. See you on Wednesday.

    The MOT booked, with a note the tester will see.
