The basics

What does Skales do?

Skales builds websites, online booking and follow-up automation for UK service businesses, and runs Meta and Google ads that send people to them. The aim is one system that turns a search, a tap or a missed call into a booked job.

Who is Skales for?

Trades and local service businesses whose work comes in by phone, message or search: plumbers and heating engineers, electricians, roofers, builders, cleaners, garages, salons, clinics, and anyone else who books work with customers.

Who will I actually deal with?

Tom Griffiths. Skales is one person, so the person you message is the person who builds it.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted flat after a short call, and agreed before anything starts. The price depends on how many pages you need, whether you take bookings or deposits, which automations you want and whether you want ads. There are no prices on this site.

How long does it take?

A website on its own takes days. A full system with booking and follow-up usually takes one to two weeks, built in public so you can watch it being made.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. The domain, the code and the accounts are set up in your name from day one. Nothing is rented back to you monthly, and if you stopped working with me, everything would keep working.

Can you guarantee I will rank first on Google?

No, and nobody honest can. What a well-built site does is give you the best chance: pages written for what your customers search, proper structured data, a fast site and a consistent Google Business Profile. Searches for your trade in your area are the ones to win first.

Are the businesses on this site real?

No. Every business, person, review and phone number in the demos and builds on this site is invented, and each one is labelled. They show how the systems work; no client is named or shown.

Can I try it before I talk to anyone?

Yes. The live demos on the Try it page run in your browser: call a business, book a job, or move a lead across a CRM board, then see what the business sees.

Websites

How long does a website take?

A website on its own takes days. A full system with booking and follow-up usually takes one to two weeks, and you can watch it being built.

How much does a website cost?

Every job is quoted flat after a short call, because the price depends on how many pages you need, whether you take bookings or deposits, and which automations you want. There are no prices on this site and no monthly fee for a page builder.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, the code and the accounts are set up in your name from day one. If you stopped working with me tomorrow, the site would keep working.

I already have a website. Will I lose my Google ranking if it is rebuilt?

Not if it is done properly. Every old address is redirected to its new page, so the links Google already knows keep working, and the new pages are faster and better structured than the old ones.

Booking and CRMThe full page

Booking and CRM

Can customers book at night or at weekends?

Yes, that is the point. The booking page shows your free slots at any hour, so a customer who finds you at 10pm books then, instead of meaning to ring you in the morning.

Can two people book the same slot?

No. A slot disappears the moment it is taken, on every device.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Booking, paying the deposit and every text they get works in the browser on their phone.

I quote rather than book. Is this still for me?

Yes. The CRM board works the same way for quotes: every enquiry gets a reply, every quote gets chased if it goes quiet, and accepted quotes become booked jobs.

Can I see where my enquiries come from?

Yes. Every enquiry is tagged with where it came from (your website, Google, a missed call, an ad), so you can see which ones turn into booked work.

Follow-up automationThe full page

Follow-up automation

What does it cost to run?

The setup is quoted flat, like everything else. After that, each text costs a few pence from the text provider.

Can I change what the messages say?

Yes. Every message is written with you before it goes live, and any of them can be changed or paused with a message to me.

Will it text my suppliers or family when I miss their calls?

Not if you do not want it to. Numbers you know can be left out, so only new callers get the text.

Do I need the website and CRM as well?

No, but they work best together. Missed-call text-back and review requests can run on their own; quote chasing and reminders need somewhere to keep the jobs, which is what the CRM is for.

Meta adsThe full page

Meta ads

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your area, your trade and how many jobs you can take on. We agree a test budget on the call, the spend goes to Meta from your own account, and you can change it at any time.

Can you guarantee leads?

No, and nobody honest can. What you do get is a clear view of what the ads cost and what they booked, so you can decide whether to keep going.

Do I need a website first?

The ads need a page built to book. If you do not have one, I build it as part of the job, and it carries on working for you whether the ads are running or not.

Facebook or Instagram?

Both run from one Meta account. Where the budget goes depends on where your customers are, and the tracking shows which one books.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. It is set up in your name like everything else, so if you stop working with me, the account, the audience data and the history stay with you.

Google adsThe full page

Google ads

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on how competitive your trade is in your area and how many jobs you want. We agree a test budget on the call, the spend goes to Google from your own account, and you can change it at any time.

How quickly do Google Ads work?

Search ads can start showing as soon as Google approves them, so they are the fastest way to appear for searches you are not ranking for yet. Getting the cost per job down takes a few weeks of tuning.

Should I do ads or SEO?

Usually both. Ads bring enquiries while the website earns its place in the normal results, which takes months. Once the site ranks for a search, you can spend less on ads for it.

Can you guarantee the top spot?

No. Where an ad appears depends on the budget, the competition and Google. What you get is a clear view of what each booked job cost.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. It is set up in your name, so the account, the data and the history stay with you.

Missed-call text-backThe full page

Missed-call text-back

Does it work with my mobile number?

Yes. Your phone forwards calls you do not answer, and the text goes out from there. You keep your number and answer calls exactly as you do now.

Will it text suppliers, family or spam callers?

Numbers you know can be left out, so only new callers get the text.

How fast does the text go?

Within seconds of the call ringing out, while the caller still has their phone in their hand.

Will it spam my customers?

No. It only texts people who have just rung you, once, about the call they made. Nobody is added to a marketing list.

Review requestsThe full page

Review requests

Can it only ask the happy customers?

No, and it should not. Google does not allow filtering out unhappy customers before asking for a review, so everyone gets asked. A business that does good work comes out of that well.

When is the best time to ask?

The same day, a couple of hours after the job, while the work is fresh and they are still pleased. The timing can be changed for your trade.

Does it work for repeat customers?

Yes. It can ask once and then leave them alone, so a regular is not asked after every visit.

Can I show my reviews on my website?

Yes. Real reviews can appear on your site, and once there are enough, in your search results too. Nothing is ever made up.

Service remindersThe full page

Service reminders

Which businesses is this for?

Any business where the work comes round: boiler services and landlord certificates, MOTs and car services, hair and beauty appointments, window and gutter cleaning, check-ups and maintenance contracts.

Can it use my existing customer list?

Yes. A spreadsheet with names, numbers and the date of their last job is enough to start.

How many reminders does a customer get?

Usually one, and one follow-up if they do not book. Then it stops until next time.

Is it allowed to text past customers?

A reminder about a service they have had before, from a business they used, is the kind of message most customers expect. Anyone who replies to opt out is never texted again.

Quote chasingThe full page

Quote chasing

Will customers find it pushy?

Not one polite message a couple of days later. It reads like a business that cares about the job, and customers often reply to say they had simply not got round to it.

How many times does it chase?

Once by default, with a second follow-up if you want one. After that the quote is left alone.

What if the customer has already accepted?

Then no chaser goes. It only follows up quotes that are still open.

Can I still send my own quotes?

Yes. You price the job; the system sends it and follows it up.

Booking depositsThe full page

Booking deposits

Will a deposit put customers off?

A small deposit on the booking page is normal for salons, clinics, restaurants and more and more trades. Customers who are serious about the job pay it; the ones who were never going to turn up tend not to book.

Where does the money go?

Straight to your own payment account, set up in your name.

What if a customer cancels?

Your rules decide. Many businesses refund with enough notice and keep the deposit for late cancellations, and the booking page says so before they pay.

Can some jobs be booked without a deposit?

Yes. You choose which jobs take one, so a free quote visit can be booked without paying anything.

On-my-way textsThe full page

On-my-way texts

Does it track my location?

No. It sends the text when you tap the button, with the arrival time you choose. Nobody is followed on a map.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Each person taps from their own job sheet, and the text says who is coming.

What about the night before?

Every booking already gets a reminder the day before. The on-my-way text is for the morning of the job.

Is this only for trades?

No. It suits anyone who goes to the customer: cleaners, mobile hairdressers, dog groomers, mechanics, surveyors.

Skales vs a page builder

Is a page builder cheaper?

At the start, usually. Over a few years the subscription and the add-ons add up, and you still do not own the site. Which works out better depends on how long you keep it and what it brings in.

Can you move my Wix or Squarespace site?

Yes. The content comes across, every old address is redirected to its new page, and the new site replaces the subscription.

Skales vs a monthly trade website

Why not just pay monthly?

If the site is a business card, a monthly plan is fine. If it is meant to bring in work, it needs pages written for your searches, booking and follow-up, and it is better owned than rented.

Can I keep my domain if I move?

Usually, yes. If the domain is in your name, it moves with you. If the provider registered it in theirs, ask them to transfer it to you first.

Skales vs a missed-call app

Is missed-call text-back worth it on its own?

Yes, a reply in the first minute is better than no reply. It is worth more when the text leads somewhere the customer can book.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your phone forwards the calls you do not answer, and you answer calls exactly as you do now.

Skales vs a directory listingThe full page

Skales vs a directory listing

Should I leave Checkatrade?

Not necessarily. Many trades use both: the directory for volume while the website builds up, and the website for the work that comes to you directly. The numbers in your CRM show when the balance has shifted.

Can my directory reviews go on my website?

You can link to them, but reviews on your own Google Business Profile are the ones that help you in search. Review requests can send every new customer there.
