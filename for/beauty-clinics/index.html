Consultation, treatment, top-up: each one booked at the right time.

A beauty or aesthetics clinic’s website needs separate booking for consultations and treatments, practitioner qualifications on show, and deposits on long or high-value appointments. It should also remind clients when their next session or top-up is due, and keep to UK advertising rules, which do not allow prescription-only medicines such as botulinum toxin to be advertised to the public.

What should a website for beauty and aesthetics clinics do?

  • Consultations and treatments booked separately, so new clients start with the right appointment.
  • Each practitioner’s qualifications and insurance, and for injectables, who prescribes.
  • Treatment pages that explain what happens, the aftercare and any patch test needed beforehand.
  • Before and after photos used only with the client’s consent, and never edited.
  • Deposits on treatments, with your cancellation rules clear before anyone pays.
Your customersHow the work comes in

How do customers choose a beauty clinic?

Clients research a treatment for a while before booking, reading about the practitioner, the products and what recovery looks like. Plenty of treatments start with a consultation, and the gap between consultation and treatment is where clients drift away. Courses and top-ups are predictable repeat work that depends on the client remembering, and no-shows on long appointments are the costliest gaps in the diary.

Beauty and aesthetics clinics: questions people ask

Can you run Facebook ads for anti-wrinkle injections?

Not for the injections themselves. Botulinum toxin is a prescription-only medicine, and UK advertising rules do not allow it to be promoted to the public, including on social media. Ads can promote a consultation with you instead, and the landing page keeps to the same rules.

Should consultations be booked separately from treatments?

Yes, for anything that needs one. New clients book a consultation first, and the treatment is booked once you have agreed it is right for them. Returning clients can book treatments directly.

Can it remind clients when they are due a top-up?

Yes. When a treatment is done, the next due date is saved, and the client gets a text ahead of it with a link to book. If they do not book, one more goes out nearer the time, and that is all.
