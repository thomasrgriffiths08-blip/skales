What it needs

What should a website for dance schools do?

A dance school’s website needs the timetable on the page itself, with each class by style, age and level, and a way for parents to book a trial class online. It should then turn a trial into a place, take term or monthly fees online and keep parents updated, because most of the admin in a dance school is chasing forms, fees and messages.

  1. 01The timetable on the page, not a PDF, with each class by style, age group and level.
  2. 02Trial classes bookable online, with a short form for the child’s age, experience and any medical notes.
  3. 03Term or monthly fees paid online, with a record of who has paid.
  4. 04Your teachers, their qualifications and any teaching body you belong to, and the DBS checks you hold.
  5. 05A place for news about shows, exams and uniform, so parents are not relying on a group chat.

Questions

Dance schools: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a dance school?

Parents usually find a dance school when their child asks to start, or when a friend’s child already goes. They want to know which class fits their child’s age, what it involves and whether there is a trial, and they ask in the evening by message. After the trial a place is offered, forms are filled in and fees start, and each step can stall if it waits on a reply. Through the year there are uniform orders, exams, shows and timetable changes, and every one of them means another round of messages.

Can parents book a trial dance class online?

Yes. They choose a class from the timetable for their child’s age and level, fill in a short form and get a confirmation with what to wear and where to go.

Can dance school fees be paid online?

Yes. Fees can be paid by card for the term or monthly, each payment lands on the family’s record, and a reminder goes to anyone who has not paid before the term starts.

What happens when a class is full?

The class shows as full and parents can join a waiting list. When a place opens, the next family is offered it by message and can take it in a tap.
