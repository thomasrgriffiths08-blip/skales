What it needs

What should a website for estate and letting agents do?

An estate or letting agent’s website is where sellers and landlords decide who to instruct, so it needs valuation booking that works out of hours, honest local evidence of what you have sold and let, and clear pages for landlord services. Letting agents in England also have to show their fees, their redress scheme and, if they hold client money, their client money protection scheme.

  1. 01Valuation booking that works at any hour, for sellers and landlords who browse in the evening.
  2. 02Recent sales and lets in your area, shown honestly, as proof you know the streets.
  3. 03Landlord pages that explain tenant find, rent collection and full management.
  4. 04For letting agents in England, your fees, redress scheme and client money protection details, where the law requires them.
  5. 05Each branch and each member of the team, with a direct way to reach them.

Questions

Estate and letting agents: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose an estate agent?

Buyers and tenants find properties on the portals, but sellers and landlords choose an agent differently: they look at who sells in their street, read reviews and check the agent’s own website before booking valuations. Calls from buyers and tenants come in all day and ring out during viewings. Work leaks when a valuation is done and nobody follows up the decision, and when the agent heading to a valuation has no quick way to say they are running late.

Do I need a website when everything is on the portals?

Buyers find houses on the portals, but sellers and landlords choose an agent, and they check your website when they do. It is the page that wins instructions, so it is the one to build properly.

What does a letting agent have to show on its website?

In England, letting agents must publish their fees, the redress scheme they belong to and, if they hold client money, their client money protection scheme. I build those in from the start, and your own compliance checks have the final say.

Can sellers book a valuation online?

Yes. They choose a time and give the address and a few details about the property, and it lands in your diary and on the CRM board. The proposal you send afterwards is followed up if it goes quiet.
