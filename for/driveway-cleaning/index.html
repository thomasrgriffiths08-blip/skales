What it needs

What should a website for driveway and patio cleaners do?

A driveway and patio cleaning website needs what you clean, from block paving and patios to decking and paths, before-and-after photos of your own jobs, and a form that takes photos so you can price without a visit. It should then follow up every quote and remind past customers when their surface is due again, because the work is seasonal and the same driveways need doing again.

  1. 01What you clean, such as block paving, patios, decking, paths and walls, and whether you re-sand and seal afterwards.
  2. 02Before-and-after photos from your own jobs, side by side.
  3. 03A quote form that asks for photos and the rough size of the area, so you can price without a visit.
  4. 04What the customer needs to do on the day, such as moving cars and pots and making sure there is an outside tap.
  5. 05The areas you cover and your insurance.

Questions

Driveway and patio cleaners: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a driveway cleaner?

Most people notice their driveway or patio in spring, when the weather turns and they want the garden ready, so enquiries bunch up just as you are busiest. They want a price for their space and often ask two or three companies. Messages arrive while the pressure washer is running and you cannot hear the phone. In the winter months work slows, and the customers you did last year will not think of you again unless you remind them.

Can I price a driveway clean from photos?

Usually. The form asks for photos and the rough size of the area, so you can reply with a price straight away and only visit for the bigger or trickier jobs.

How do I get last year’s customers back?

Remind them. A message at the interval you suggested, sent before the season starts, lets them book again in a minute, and their old job and photos are already on their record.

Is pressure washing the same as driveway cleaning?

To most customers, yes. People search for driveway cleaning, patio cleaning and pressure washing, so the website should use all three and have a page for each surface you clean.
