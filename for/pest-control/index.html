A page for every pest people type into Google.

A pest control website needs a page for each pest, because people search for wasps, rats or bed bugs rather than for pest control, and each page should say how you treat it and what the visit involves. It also needs to answer urgent calls fast, keep every message discreet, and look after commercial contracts whose routine visits have to happen on time.

What it needsPest control

What should a website for pest control do?

  • Separate pages for wasps, rats, mice, bed bugs, moths and birds, each saying how you deal with it.
  • What happens on the visit, including any return visits and what is safe around children and pets.
  • Whether you offer discreet, unmarked visits, said clearly for the customers who want them.
  • A commercial page for food businesses, landlords and offices, with contracts and routine visits explained.
  • A call button for urgent jobs, and a form for the ones that can wait a day.
Your customersHow the work comes in

How do customers choose a pest controller?

Pest problems are urgent and a little embarrassing, so people search for the pest by name and ring the first local firm that seems to know what it is doing. Plenty want discretion: an unmarked van, a text that does not say rats. Commercial customers are different again, wanting a contract, routine visits and paperwork to show an inspector, and those visits slip when the schedule lives in someone’s head.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Pest control: questions people ask

Should each pest have its own page?

Yes. People search for the pest, often with the town, so a page about wasp nests is the one found by someone with a wasp nest. Each page should say how you treat it and what the visit involves.

Can the texts be discreet?

Yes. Every message is written with you, so they can say who is coming and when without naming the pest or the problem.

Can it keep track of commercial contract visits?

Yes. Each contract’s next visit is saved in the CRM, the customer gets a reminder before it, and the visit goes in your diary like any other job.
