What it needsCar valeting and detailing

What should a website for car valeting and detailing do?

  • Your packages, with what each one includes and how long it takes, adjusted for the size of the car.
  • Online booking, with a deposit on the long jobs such as machine polishing and ceramic coating.
  • Before and after photos of your own work, sorted by the kind of job.
  • Whether you are mobile or work from a unit, the area you cover, and anything you need on site such as water or power.
  • What happens if the weather turns, for work done outside.

How do customers choose a car valeter?

People choose a valeter or detailer on the photos: the before and after, on cars like theirs. Then they want to know what each package includes and whether you will come to them. The work leaks when quotes for bigger details go quiet, when a full-day booking is cancelled the night before, and when a customer who loved the result is never asked to book a maintenance wash.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Car valeting and detailing: questions people ask

Should a car detailer take deposits?

For the long jobs, yes. A deposit at booking holds the day, your terms are shown before anyone pays, and it can come off the final price. Shorter valets can be booked without one if you prefer.

How do customers book the right package for their car?

The booking asks for the size of the car first, then shows each package at the length and price you set for that size. The customer sees what they will pay before they pick a time.

What if it rains on a booked day?

Your weather policy goes on the website and in the booking confirmation. If a job has to move, the customer gets a text with a link to pick a new slot, so nobody has to be chased by phone.
