What it needs

What should a website for interior designers do?

An interior designer’s website needs finished projects shown room by room, a plain explanation of how you work and what each service includes, and a way to book a first consultation online. It should then follow up every design proposal, keep the client informed as the project moves, and ask for a review once the room is finished and photographed.

  1. 01Projects shown room by room, with the brief, the before and the finished space.
  2. 02Each service explained plainly: a single consultation, a room design, or a whole project.
  3. 03First consultations booked online, in person or on a video call.
  4. 04A short form before the consultation: the rooms, the timescale and photos of the space.
  5. 05Your area and the kinds of project you take on, stated clearly.

Questions

Interior designers: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose an interior designer?

Clients usually find a designer through photos, then look for work that matches the kind of home they have and the style they want. Before they get in touch they want to know how you work, whether you take on single rooms or whole houses, and what a first consultation involves. The leaks are enquiries that ask for a price before a conversation, proposals sent and never followed up, and finished projects that never become reviews.

Can clients book a first consultation online?

Yes. They choose a time in person or on a video call, and send the rooms, timescale and photos before it.

Can I charge for a consultation when it is booked?

Yes. A paid consultation is taken by card at the time of booking, and a reminder goes out before it.

Are design proposals followed up?

Yes. Each proposal is followed up after a few days, so it does not go quiet while the client decides.
