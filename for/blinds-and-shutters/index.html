What it needs

What should a website for blinds and shutters do?

A blinds and shutters company’s website needs photos of fitted rooms, the products you offer, from roller and Roman blinds to shutters and curtains, and a way to book a free measuring visit online. It should then follow up every quote after the visit and ask for a review once the fitting is done, because customers often get more than one quote and choose the company that stays in touch.

  1. 01Photos of fitted rooms from your own jobs, by product and by room, with a line on what was chosen.
  2. 02The products you offer: roller, Roman, Venetian and vertical blinds, shutters, curtains and motorised options.
  3. 03Online booking for a free measuring visit, at times that fit around work, including evenings or Saturdays if you offer them.
  4. 04What happens after the visit: the quote, the lead time for made-to-measure orders and the fitting day.
  5. 05The areas you cover, and your showroom with its opening hours if you have one.

Questions

Blinds and shutters: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a blinds company?

People look for blinds or shutters when they move, redecorate or tire of a window that faces the street. They browse photos for ideas, then book a home visit to see samples and be measured, often with two companies. After the visit comes a quote, and it can sit while they decide on fabric, colour or budget. Made-to-measure orders then take a few weeks before fitting, and customers want to know where their order is.

Can customers book a blinds measuring visit online?

Yes. They choose a time, say which rooms and products they are thinking about, and get a confirmation and a reminder the day before.

How do I follow up quotes after a home visit?

The quote is sent from the board, and a friendly follow-up goes a few days later if there is no answer. It stops the moment the customer accepts or replies.

Can I take a deposit when a customer accepts a quote?

Yes. The acceptance link can take a deposit by card, so the order can be placed the same day and the fitting booked once it arrives.
