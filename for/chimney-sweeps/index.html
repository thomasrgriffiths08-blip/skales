What it needs

What should a website for chimney sweeps do?

A chimney sweep’s website needs the appliances you sweep, from open fires to wood burners, the areas you cover and any trade body you belong to, with sweeps bookable online. It should then send the certificate after the job and remind each customer when their next sweep is due, because the same chimneys come round every year and the autumn rush goes to whoever is easiest to book.

  1. 01The appliances you sweep, such as open fires, wood burners and multi-fuel stoves, and the areas you cover.
  2. 02Any trade body you belong to and your insurance, shown where customers look before they book.
  3. 03Sweeps bookable online, with a short form about the appliance, the fuel it burns and access to the chimney.
  4. 04A certificate after each sweep, emailed to the customer and kept on their record.
  5. 05A line on what to do before you arrive, such as letting the fire go out and clearing the hearth.

Questions

Chimney sweeps: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a chimney sweep?

Most people think about their chimney when the weather turns, so bookings bunch up in the autumn and arrive by phone while you are on a roof or under a dust sheet. In spring and summer the diary goes quiet. The customers are the same households year after year, but they drift to whoever put a leaflet through the door last unless someone reminds them, and the certificate they keep for their records ends up as a scrap of paper in a drawer.

Can customers book a chimney sweep online?

Yes. They pick the appliance and a time from the slots you open, answer a few questions about the fuel and access, and get a confirmation that says what to do before you arrive.

Can the sweep certificate be sent automatically?

Yes. The certificate is filled in on your phone at the end of the job and emailed to the customer straight away, and a copy stays on their record for next year.

How do I fill the quiet months?

Reminders do most of it. Each customer hears from you when their sweep is due, timed before the autumn rush so the booking lands in weeks that would otherwise be empty, and you can open extra slots in those weeks for them.
