What should a website for carpet and upholstery cleaners do?

A carpet cleaner’s website needs what you clean, from carpets and stairs to sofas and rugs, a simple way to choose the rooms and items, and online booking with a deposit for the slot. It should then remind each customer when their next clean is due and ask for a review with before-and-after photos, because carpet cleaning is a job people come back for and recommend.

  3. 03Online booking with a deposit, and a line on drying times and what to move before you arrive.

Questions

Carpet and upholstery cleaners: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a carpet cleaner?

People look for a carpet cleaner when they are moving out, getting ready for guests, or dealing with a spill or a pet accident. Landlords and letting agents want end-of-tenancy cleans on a tight date. Most customers want to know what it will cost for their rooms before they book, and they ask by message or phone while you are mid-job with the machine running. Once the carpets are clean they forget about you until next time, unless you remind them.

Can customers book a carpet clean online?

Yes. They choose the rooms and items, pick a time from the slots you open, pay a deposit and get a confirmation with what to move and how long things take to dry.

Can customers see a price before they book?

If you price by room or item, the booking form can show it as they choose. If you prefer to see the job first, the form asks for photos and you reply with a price.

How do I get landlords and letting agents to book me?

Give them their own page and form. They usually need a clean on a set date between tenants, and a quick way to book, pay and get an invoice makes you the easy choice next time.
