What it needsHair salons and barbers

What should a website for hair salons and barbers do?

  • Booking by stylist or barber, with each service taking the time it really takes.
  • Deposits on colour, extensions and long appointments, with your cancellation rules shown before they pay.
  • A patch test appointment before a first colour, built into the booking so it is not forgotten.
  • Your price list on the page, by stylist level if that is how you charge.
  • For barbers, whether you take walk-ins, bookings or both, and when.
Your customersHow the work comes in

How do customers choose a salon?

Clients find a salon through friends, Instagram and the map, then want to book there and then, often late in the evening. They are loyal to a stylist as much as to a salon, so booking has to start with who they want. Work leaks through no-shows on long colour appointments, calls missed mid-cut, and clients who mean to rebook and drift somewhere else.

Hair salons and barbers: questions people ask

Can clients choose their stylist when they book?

Yes. They pick the stylist first, then the service and a time that stylist has free. Each service takes the time you set, so a trim and a full colour are never squeezed into the same slot.

What about walk-ins at a barbers?

The site says when you take walk-ins and when you take bookings, and the booking page only shows the times you want booked. Walk-in hours stay walk-in.

Will regulars who like to ring still be able to?

Yes, nothing changes for them. If you miss their call they get a text with the booking link, and they can use it or wait for you to ring back.
