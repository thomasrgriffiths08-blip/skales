What it needs

What should a website for rubbish removal and clearance do?

A rubbish removal website needs your waste carrier registration number on show, with a link to the public register, because householders are responsible for checking it. It should let customers send photos for a quote, book a collection time online and see plainly what you will and will not take.

  1. 01Your waste carrier registration number, with a link to the public register so customers can check it.
  2. 02A quote form that takes photos, the address and access details, so you can price without a visit.
  3. 03Collections bookable online into the slots you have, with the address and parking notes taken at booking.
  4. 04What you take and what you do not, such as paint, asbestos or gas bottles, so nobody is surprised on the day.
  5. 05Where the waste goes, how much is reused or recycled, and the paperwork a business customer gets.

Questions

Rubbish removal and clearance: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a rubbish removal firm?

Clearance jobs come from a house move, a renovation, a garden tidy or an empty property, and the customer usually wants it gone this week. They want a rough price before anyone visits, so they send photos to a few firms and go with the one that answers first and looks legitimate. The leaks are quotes that go unanswered, calls missed while you are loading the van, and customers who cannot tell a registered carrier from a fly-tipper.

Should my waste carrier number be on my website?

Yes, with a link to the public register. Householders are legally responsible for making sure their waste goes to a registered carrier, so a careful customer will look for it before they book.

Can customers get a quote from photos?

Yes. The quote form takes photos straight from their phone with the address and access details, and the enquiry lands on your CRM board ready to price.

Can collections be booked online?

Yes. Once a quote is accepted the customer picks a collection slot you have free, and it goes into your diary with the address and notes.
