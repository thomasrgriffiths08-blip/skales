What it needs

What should a website for personal trainers do?

A personal trainer’s website needs to say who you train and how, with your qualifications and real photos, and let people book a first consultation in a couple of taps. After that it should take payment for sessions or blocks at booking, so late cancellations and no-shows stop costing you the hour.

  1. 01Who you train and what you help with, from strength and weight loss to rehab or sport, in your own words.
  2. 02Your qualifications, and where you train: a gym, a private studio, outdoors, online or in clients’ homes.
  3. 03A first consultation bookable online, with a short form about goals, injuries and experience.
  4. 04Sessions and blocks paid for at booking, with your cancellation policy shown first.
  5. 05Real photos of you training people, not stock images of someone else’s gym.

Questions

Personal trainers: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a personal trainer?

People hire a personal trainer they feel they can work with, so they read about you, look at who else you train, and message before they commit. That first message often arrives while you are with a client, and by the evening they have asked someone else. Once they start, the work leaks through sessions cancelled an hour before and blocks that run out with nobody suggesting the next one.

Should a personal trainer take payment up front?

For blocks and regular sessions, yes. Clients pay when they book, your cancellation policy is shown before they do, and a session cancelled inside your notice period is covered by the rules you set rather than lost.

Do I need a website if I find clients on Instagram?

Instagram shows your work, but a website is where someone ready to start can read about you, check your qualifications and book a consultation without waiting for a reply. It is also what turns up when people search for a trainer near them.

Can clients book online sessions as well as in-person ones?

Yes. Each kind of session has its own length, place and booking rules, and online sessions send the video link in the confirmation and the reminder.
