What it needs

What should a website for roofers do?

A roofer’s website has to prove what customers cannot check for themselves: real photos of your own work before and after, a fixed business address, and how your guarantee works. It also needs a fast way to report a leak and a quote process that follows up, because re-roofs are compared and a storm brings every call at once.

  1. 01Before and after photos of your own roofs, sorted by job: pitched, flat, chimneys, fascias and guttering.
  2. 02A leak and storm damage call button at the top of every page on a phone.
  3. 03A real business address and a photo of you and the van, so nobody mistakes you for a doorstep caller.
  4. 04Your guarantee and insurance set out in plain words, not a badge in the footer.
  5. 05A guide price tool built on your own rates, if you want customers to arrive knowing roughly what to expect.

Questions

Roofers: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a roofer?

Roofing customers cannot see the problem and have usually been warned about doorstep roofers, so they look hard at who they ring. After a storm the phone goes all day, and calls missed on the scaffold are calls lost. A re-roof is a big decision priced against other firms, and the quote that comes with photos, a clear explanation and a follow-up is the one that stays in the running.

How do I show customers I’m not a cowboy?

Real photos of your own roofs, a business address, your guarantee in writing and reviews from real local customers. Stock photos of perfect roofs do the opposite.

Should my website give prices?

It can give a range, if you want it to. A guide price tool works from your own rates and the customer’s roof, and makes clear that the final price follows a survey.

Will the website cope when a storm hits and everyone rings at once?

The site will, because it is hand-built and fast. The phone is the bottleneck, so missed calls get a text back asking for the address and what they can see, and you work through them in order.
