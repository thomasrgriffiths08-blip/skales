What it needs

What should a website for garages and MOT centres do?

A garage website needs MOTs and services bookable online into the slots you actually have, with the registration taken at booking, clear prices for the common jobs and your opening hours. It then needs reminders that go out before each customer’s MOT and service fall due, and a way to follow up the advisories from the last test.

  1. 01MOT and service booking online, showing only the ramp slots you really have, with the registration taken at booking.
  2. 02That you are a DVSA-approved MOT test centre, and the classes of vehicle you test.
  3. 03Your prices for MOTs, services and common jobs, set out plainly.
  4. 04Whether customers can wait, drop off or have the car collected, and your opening hours.
  5. 05A page for each job people search for: MOTs, servicing, diagnostics, tyres, brakes and air conditioning.

Questions

Garages and MOT centres: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a garage?

MOTs are searched for with a deadline in mind, and a garage that lets drivers pick a time online saves them a phone call on their lunch break. Once a car has been in, the next MOT and service are predictable, yet customers drift to whoever reminds them first. Advisories are the other leak: the work is noted at the test and then forgotten until it becomes a fail.

Can customers book an MOT online and pick a time?

Yes. They choose the MOT or service, then a free slot on your ramp, and give their registration. Taken slots disappear, so the ramp is never double booked.

Will it remind customers when their MOT is due?

Yes. The due date is saved when the car comes in, and the customer gets a text before it with a link to book, plus a single nudge closer to the date. You can start from your existing customer list.

Can I follow up MOT advisories?

Yes. Price the advisory work and send it as a quote from the CRM, and if the customer does not reply a polite follow-up goes out. Accepted work goes straight into the diary.
