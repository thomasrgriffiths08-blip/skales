What should a website for locksmiths do?

A locksmith’s website has to prove two things fast to someone standing outside a locked door: that you are a real local locksmith rather than a call centre, and that they can reach you now. That means your name, face, van and area on the page, a call button that never leaves the screen, and a text back to any call you miss.

  1. 01A call button fixed to the bottom of the screen on a phone, for the customer on the doorstep.
  2. 02Your name, a photo of you and the van, and the area you cover, so customers know who is coming.
  3. 03How you open doors without damage where you can, and that the price is agreed before you start.
  4. 04Insurance-approved locks explained, such as BS 3621 mortice locks, for customers whose insurer asks for them.
  5. 05Separate pages for lockouts, lock changes, uPVC door repairs, landlord changeovers and commercial work.

Questions

Locksmiths: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a locksmith?

Lockouts are searched for on a phone, on the doorstep, often in the dark, and the customer rings the first number that looks local. Many have heard of call centres that pass the job to whoever is free, so a real name, a real address and clear terms count for a lot. The rest of the work, lock changes after a burglary or a house move, upgrades for insurance and landlord changeovers, is planned and easily lost when the phone is the only way in.

How do I show I’m local and not a call centre?

Your own name, a photo of you and the van, a business address and the towns you cover, all on the page. Leave out stock photos and anything that reads like a national network.

Is it worth a page for every town I cover?

Only for places you genuinely cover, and only if each page says something real about working there. Thin copies of the same page with the town name swapped can do more harm than good with Google.

Does the missed-call text work in the middle of the night?

Yes, it goes out whatever the hour. What it says is up to you, so at night it can say when you can get there, or that you will ring back first thing.
