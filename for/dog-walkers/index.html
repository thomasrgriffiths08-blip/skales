What it needs

What should a website for dog walkers and pet sitters do?

A dog walker’s website needs the area you cover, the services you offer, from group and solo walks to pet sitting and home boarding, and a way for a new owner to book a meet-and-greet first. After that it should let regulars book and change walks online and tell owners when you are on your way, because the work is built on trust and on the same dogs every week.

  1. 01The postcodes and areas you cover, so owners outside them do not have to ask.
  2. 02Each service set out plainly: group walks, solo walks, puppy visits, cat and pet sitting, and home boarding or day care if you offer them.
  3. 03A meet-and-greet bookable online, with a form for the dog’s age, health, vet and how it gets on with other dogs.
  4. 04Your insurance, and any council licence you hold for home boarding or day care.
  5. 05Regular walks booked by the week, with extra walks and holiday cover requested from the same page.

Questions

Dog walkers and pet sitters: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a dog walker?

Owners look for a walker when they go back to the office, get a puppy or book a holiday, and they want someone local they can trust with a key and a dog. They check the area, read about you and want to meet before anything else. After that the work is regular, but it gets messy when weekly walks are arranged by text, holiday cover is asked for at the last minute, and a new enquiry sits unanswered while you are out with a van full of dogs.

Can owners book dog walks online?

Yes. New owners book a meet-and-greet first, and once you have taken a dog on, its owner books regular walks, extra walks and holiday cover from the same page, only into the times you have open.

Do I need a website if I get dogs through an app?

An app lists you beside every other walker nearby. Your own website is where an owner who has heard of you can read about you, see the area you cover and book a meet-and-greet with you directly.

Does it work for pet sitting and home boarding too?

Yes. Sitting visits and boarding stays are booked by date rather than by time, and the form takes what you need for each, such as feeding, keys and medication.
