What it needsWedding venues

What should a website for wedding venues do?

  • Photos of each space set for the ceremony and the reception, in daylight and in the evening, and across the seasons.
  • Capacities for the ceremony, the meal and the evening, and what is included, written plainly.
  • A way to ask about a date that gets an answer the same day, not days later.
  • Online booking for show rounds and open days.
  • Rooms for guests, parking, access and places to stay nearby.
Your customersHow the work comes in

How do customers choose a wedding venue?

Couples start looking soon after they get engaged, often a year or more before the date. They shortlist venues from photos, send enquiries to several at once, usually in the evening or at the weekend, and ask about dates, numbers and what is included. The next step is a show round, and after that a decision that can take weeks. Once they book, there are menus, numbers, suppliers and timings to settle, and a steady stream of messages in the months before the day.

Wedding venues: questions people ask

Can couples book a wedding venue show round online?

Yes. They choose a time for a show round or an open day, give their rough date and numbers, and get a confirmation and a reminder before they visit.

How do I stop losing couples who enquire at several venues?

Reply fast and keep in touch. The enquiry gets an answer straight away with your brochure and a link to book a show round, and a friendly follow-up goes later if they go quiet.

Can a deposit be taken online to hold a wedding date?

Yes. When a couple decides, they pay the deposit by card from a link, and the date shows as held in your diary at that moment.
