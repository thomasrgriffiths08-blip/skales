Eye tests booked online, and every recall sent on time.

An optician’s website needs eye tests bookable online, the services you offer, from NHS and private tests to contact lenses and specialist clinics, and photos of the practice and the frames you stock. It should then remind each patient when their next test is due and tell them when their glasses are ready, because most patients will not remember the date themselves, and the practice that reminds them is the one they come back to.

What it needsOpticians

What should a website for opticians do?

  • Eye tests bookable online, with NHS and private options and times that include any evenings or Saturdays you open.
  • The services you offer: eye tests, contact lenses, children’s tests, dry eye or other clinics, and home visits if you do them.
  • Photos of the practice and the frame ranges you stock, so people know what to expect before they visit.
  • Your optometrists and dispensing opticians, with their GOC registration.
  • Who can have an NHS sight test, in plain words, and what to bring to the appointment.
Your customersHow the work comes in

How do customers choose an optician?

People look for an optician when their eyes change, their glasses break, or a reminder arrives from their last practice. Many choose between a high street chain and an independent nearby, and they look at the practice, the frames and whether they can book at a time that suits them. After the test comes choosing frames, a wait for the glasses and a trip to collect them. Then nothing happens until the next test is due, often a year or two later, unless something reminds them.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

Opticians: questions people ask

Can patients book an eye test online?

Yes. They choose NHS or private, the type of test and a time, and get a confirmation and a reminder the day before.

How do recall reminders work for opticians?

Each patient keeps the date their next test is due. A message goes when it is close, with a link to book, and another later if they have not booked.

Can patients be told when their glasses are ready?

Yes. When the order arrives, the patient gets a message saying it is ready to collect, with your opening hours, so glasses do not sit uncollected on the shelf.
