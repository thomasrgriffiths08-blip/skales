What it needs

What should a website for removal companies do?

A removal company’s website needs a quote form that asks for both addresses, the size of the move, access and the date if there is one, plus a way to book a home or video survey. Behind it, quotes need following up patiently, because the customer’s date depends on a chain, and a deposit should hold the date once it is fixed.

  1. 01A quote form that asks for both addresses, property sizes, access and the move date, or whether it is still unknown.
  2. 02Home and video surveys bookable online, at times that suit people who work.
  3. 03Separate pages for packing, storage, single items and office moves.
  4. 04Your goods in transit insurance and what it covers, in plain words.
  5. 05The area you cover, and whether you take long-distance moves.

Questions

Removal companies: questions people ask

More in every question, answered.

How do customers choose a removals firm?

Moving home is stressful and dates move, so customers ask for quotes early and decide late. They compare firms on how carefully the move was surveyed, what the insurance covers and what other movers say. Work leaks when a quote goes out and nobody checks back after the chain moves, and when a date agreed on the phone is never actually secured.

What if the customer’s completion date changes?

You move the booking to the new date. Your rules decide what happens to the deposit if the date changes or the move falls through, and the booking page says so before they pay.

Can people book a video survey online?

Yes. They choose the survey type and a free slot from your diary, and the survey lands in your diary with both addresses and their notes.

How much should the quote form ask?

Enough to price the move or decide it needs a survey: addresses, property sizes, access and the date. Anything more can wait for the survey, because a long form puts people off before they send it.
