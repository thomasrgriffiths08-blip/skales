Emergency electrician

Burning smell, sparks, or the whole house off?

Ring the emergency line. It is Rob's mobile, answered by Rob, at any hour. At your door within 90 minutes anywhere in NG1 to NG17. £120 out of hours, including the first hour.

Emergency line · 24 hours07700 900 417

  1. Switch off at the main switch

    If you can reach it safely and the board is dry. If there is water near it, stay away from it.

  2. Do not touch anything hot

    Sockets or plugs that are warm or brown round the edges: leave them unplugged and unused.

  3. Ring us, then wait outside if you smell burning

    If there is smoke or flame, ring 999 first. We will talk you through the rest on the phone.
