      Questions

      In the order ops directors ask them.

      If yours is not here, put it to us on the walkthrough. We would rather lose the deal early than in month four.

      Do we have to replace our field service system?

      No, and we would talk you out of it. Cadence reads from your FSM over its API and writes back only the status changes you approve. Twenty-three of our twenty-seven deployments still run the FSM they had before us.

      What happens if the risk model is wrong?

      It proposes, you decide. Cadence never moves a crew on its own unless you turn that on for a named contract. Every proposal shows its inputs and the cost of both choices, and coordinators overrule it about 12% of the time in month one and 4% by month three.

      Our engineers hate new apps.

      Fair. The field app is three taps and works with no signal. If adoption is under 60% by week three of the pilot, we come on site and find out why rather than emailing a training deck.

      Where does our data live, and who can see it?

      UK South by default, with a Frankfurt region for EU groups. Encrypted at rest and in transit, a single-tenant database per customer, SSO on every plan. Our engineers cannot read your data without a time-boxed grant that shows in your audit log.

      What does the pilot cost if it fails?

      Nothing for the month. The pilot is one month at your plan price on your real data, with one region held back as the control. If the pilot region does not beat it on late completions, we cancel the invoice.
