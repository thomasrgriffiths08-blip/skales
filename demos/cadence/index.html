02 / FILTER STATES

The rescore loop

Five checks, run in this order,
every ninety seconds.

No modules to buy, no per-seat maths. Every account gets all five from day one — the difference between plans is volume, and how deep we go into your finance stack.

01

The job tells you it's slipping. Not the customer.

status:slipping

Cadence reads engineer check-ins, parts movements, van telematics and the customer's own access windows, then scores every live job for slip risk every 90 seconds. When a score crosses your threshold it doesn't email someone — it proposes the swap, prices the penalty either way, and waits for one tap.

Rescore 90sInputs 4 sourcesException routing

02

Live cost, not month-end.

margin:live

Labour, travel, parts and subcontract land against the job as they happen, priced off your own rate cards. You know which jobs are underwater on the day you can still fix them, not on the twelfth of next month when the ledger closes.

Lag < 4 minRate cards yoursMargin per job

03

Two taps, offline, gloves on.

signal:none

Engineers get the next job, the asset history and a photo-first sign-off. Four screens, no training deck. It queues and syncs when the signal comes back, which on this customer base means plant rooms, chill stores and the bottom of a lift shaft.

Screens 4Offline queue unlimitedField app

04

Invoice raised before the van is home.

ledger:write

Signed-off jobs push straight into Sage, Xero or NetSuite with the evidence pack attached — photos, timestamps, parts used, the customer's signature. Average time from sign-off to invoice at our customers fell from nine days to thirty-one hours.

Sage · Xero · NetSuiteWas 9 daysNow 31 hrs

05

Argue with the recording, not each other.

replay:*

Every board state is kept. Replay any day, see who moved what and when the slip actually started. Three weeks of "whose fault was it" collapses into four minutes of scrubbing a timeline in front of the client.

Retention unlimitedExport audit CSVProgramme replay

We plug into what you already run.

Read-only connectors into your FSM, telematics, parts stock and accounting ledger. Nothing is migrated and nothing is switched off, so the failure mode of a bad week is that you carry on exactly as you did before.
