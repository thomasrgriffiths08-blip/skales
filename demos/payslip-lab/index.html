Caveats worth reading
  • Ignores accountancy, admin and company-formation costs for the limited company route.
  • Assumes all company profit is extracted as salary and dividend in the same tax year — nothing retained.
  • Employment Allowance is not applied — most single-director-only companies cannot claim it against employer NI.
  • Employer pension contributions aren't modelled for the limited company route; only personal contributions are.
  • Sole trader Class 2 National Insurance is treated as abolished — voluntary contributions for state pension credit aren't modelled.
  • The tax code override in the sidebar only applies to the employee scenario — sole trader and Ltd use the standard personal allowance.
  • The dividend nil-rate band is approximated as a flat £500 credit at the marginal dividend rate.
  • Student loan repayment is applied to all three routes using your sidebar plan: on salary for the employee route, on profit for the sole trader route, and on total salary + dividends (the self-assessment basis) for the limited company route.
