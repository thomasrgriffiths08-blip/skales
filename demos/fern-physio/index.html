Your first appointment

Forty-five minutes, minute by minute

Nobody should pay for something without knowing what it is. This is the whole first visit, in order, whichever of us you see.

  1. 0 min

    You talk, we listen

    What happened, when it started, what makes it worse and what makes it easier. No clipboard while you are trying to explain.

  2. 5 min

    The rest of the picture

    Work, sport, sleep, old injuries, anything you are taking. Half of what we need is in the history rather than the joint.

  3. 12 min

    We watch you move

    Walking, squatting, reaching, hopping: whatever the painful thing actually uses. Bring the shoes you run or work in.

  4. 20 min

    Hands-on testing

    Strength side to side and joint range measured with a goniometer. It goes on paper as a number, so the next visit has something to beat.

  5. 30 min

    Treatment starts today

    Manual therapy, taping or dry needling if it fits the problem. You do not come back a second time to start being treated.

  6. 38 min

    Your programme

    Three or four exercises, written down with sets, reps and the load. Not a login to an app you will never open again.

  7. 45 min

    What happens next

    How many visits we think it needs, what would change our mind, and when to ring rather than wait.

Before you come

  • Wear or bring shorts if it is a leg, a vest top if it is a shoulder.
  • Bring any letters or scans from a GP or consultant, if you have them.
  • Arrive five minutes early. Ring the bell at 14a and one of us comes down.
  • Paying: card or cash on the day. Insured? Bring your authorisation number.
  • Changing your mind: no fee with 24 hours' notice, just ring or reply to the text.
