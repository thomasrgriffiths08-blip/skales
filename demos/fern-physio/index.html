Your first appointment

Forty-five minutes, minute by minute

The whole first visit, in order, whichever of us you see.

  1. 0 min

    You talk, we listen

    What happened, when it started, what makes it worse. No clipboard while you explain.

  2. 5 min

    The rest of the picture

    Work, sport, sleep and old injuries. Half of what we need is in the history.

  3. 12 min

    We watch you move

    Walking, squatting, reaching: whatever the painful thing uses. Bring your shoes.

  4. 20 min

    Hands-on testing

    Strength side to side and range with a goniometer, written down as numbers to beat.

  5. 30 min

    Treatment starts today

    Manual therapy, taping or dry needling if it fits. No second visit just to start.

  6. 38 min

    Your programme

    Three or four exercises with sets, reps and load, on paper. Not an app login.

  7. 45 min

    What happens next

    How many visits we expect, what would change our mind, and when to ring.
