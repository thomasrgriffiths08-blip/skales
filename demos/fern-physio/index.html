The first appointment

Forty-five minutes, minute by minute

Nobody should pay for something and not know what it is. This is the whole first visit, in order, and it is the same whichever of us you see.

0 min

You talk, we listen

What happened, when it started, what makes it worse and what makes it easier. No forms on a clipboard while you are trying to explain.

5 min

The rest of the picture

Work, sport, sleep, previous injuries, anything you are taking. Half of what we need is in the history rather than the joint.

12 min

We watch you move

Walking, squatting, reaching overhead, hopping — whatever the painful thing actually uses. Bring the shoes you run or work in.

20 min

Hands-on testing

Strength side to side, joint range measured with a goniometer, and it goes on paper as a number so the next visit has something to beat.

30 min

Treatment starts today

Manual therapy, taping or dry needling if it fits the problem. You do not come back a second time to start being treated.

38 min

Your programme

Three or four exercises, written down with sets, reps and the load. Not a login to an app you will never open again.

45 min

What happens next

How many visits we think it needs, what would make us change our mind, and when to ring rather than wait for the next appointment.

The clinic

Two physios. That is the whole clinic.
