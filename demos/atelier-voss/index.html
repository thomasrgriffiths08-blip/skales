05 · The practice

A building outlives the argument that made it.

Eleven of us, in a grain loft on the shore at Leith. We work in concrete, timber and the light that falls between them, and we take on about six new projects a year so that every one is drawn by the person who'll be on site.

41buildings finished since 2009
11people, one studio
6new projects a year
  1. 01Standing

    A week on your site before a single line is drawn: where the light lands, where the wind comes from.

  2. 02Drawing

    By hand at 1:50 first, then the full set. You see every sheet the builder will see.

  3. 03Making

    Samples before specification. Every concrete mix is cast three times in the yard.

  4. 04Keeping

    We come back at one, five and ten years, and fix what the building has told us.
