    The venue

    Find us
    Under the railway, by the river

    Unit 3, Cardigan Mills,
    Kirkstall Road,
    Leeds LS4 2AW
    Phone 0113 496 0733 WhatsApp 07700 900 733 Email southpaw@example.co.uk Buses 33, 33A and 34 stop outside. Burley Park station is a nine-minute walk. Parking Free in the mill yard after 17:30 and all weekend.
    • 1Turn up 15 minutes early. Give your name at the desk; your wraps are in a bag with it on.
    • 2A coach wraps your hands. Then stance, guard and the jab, in front of a mirror, for twenty minutes.
    • 3Bags, then pads. Three rounds each, on the club clock. You'll hear the bell.
    • 4No hard sell. Prices are on the wall and on this page. Join at the desk or don't.
