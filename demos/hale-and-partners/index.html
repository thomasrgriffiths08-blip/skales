Sheet 4a · How it works

How we work

  • A fixed fee, agreed in writing before anything starts, paid monthly if that suits you better.
  • One of the four of us on your account, by name, and you have their direct line.
  • Records in by the date we agree; draft accounts back to you inside four weeks.
  • We ring you in the February before your year end, not the October after it.
  • If you leave, your records go with you in a week, in a format your next accountant can open.
  • No commission from anybody. If we recommend something, that's the only reason we're recommending it.
