The flash wall

Nine sheets pinned up this month. Scroll and they ink themselves — purple stencil to black, the same way it happens on your arm.

Every design here is drawn by the artist whose name is on it and tattooed once. Sizes are a guide; the artist will tell you honestly if a design won't hold at the size you want. Tap a sheet for sizes and to add it to a consultation.

The residents

Three people, three line weights. The drawing on each sheet is set in that artist's own weight — it is the quickest honest way to see the difference.

Fresh, then a year on

Fine line settles and spreads a little. Bold line stays where it's put. This is the same design on the day it's done and twelve months later — and it is the honest reason we will talk you out of 3cm lettering with six words in it.

Straight off the machine: the line is at its crispest and the skin around it is raised.

The studio

One room, three chairs, a door that locks. Nobody watches you get tattooed unless you want them to.

What happens at a consultation

  1. Twenty minutes with the artist you'd be booking. Bring photos, scribbles, the thing off your nan's teapot — anything is more use than a description.
  2. We talk placement and size on your actual arm, with a paper cut-out, because 10cm is always smaller than people think.
  3. You leave with a date, a price and a £40 deposit paid. The drawing gets done in the fortnight before you come in, and you see it on the day.

House rules

  • Over 18s. Photo ID every time, even if we tattooed you last month.
  • No drink, no drugs, no coming in on an empty stomach.
  • One friend in the waiting room, nobody in the chair room.
  • More than 15 minutes late and the deposit goes; we'll rebook you, but the £40's gone.
  • We don't tattoo hands, necks or faces on a first sitting.

Aftercare, in five lines

  1. Film stays on for four hours. Take it off in a warm shower, not dry.
  2. Wash twice a day with unscented soap, cold rinse, pat dry with kitchen roll.
  3. A thin smear of the balm we give you — thin. It needs air, not a blanket.
  4. No gym, no baths, no swimming, no sunbeds for two weeks. Sea and pools: three.
  5. It will flake and it will itch. Do not pick it. Ring us if it's hot or weeping.
