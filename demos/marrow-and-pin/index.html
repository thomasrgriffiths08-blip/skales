How booking works

  1. A free consultation

    Twenty minutes with the artist you'd be booking. Bring photos, scribbles, the thing off your nan's teapot. We size it on your arm with a paper cut-out.

  2. A date and a £40 deposit

    You leave with a date and a price. The deposit comes off the price on the day.

  3. The day

    The drawing's ready, the stencil goes on and you check it in the mirror. Needles and ink caps are single use and opened in front of you. Nobody watches unless you want them to.

  • Over 18s. Photo ID every time.
  • No drink, no drugs, and eat before you come in.
  • More than 15 minutes late and the deposit goes.
  • No hands, necks or faces on a first sitting.

Aftercare, in five lines

  1. Film off after four hours, in a warm shower.
  2. Wash it twice a day with plain soap.
  3. A thin smear of balm. Thin.
  4. No swimming or sunbeds for two weeks.
  5. Hot or weeping? Ring us.

You get all of this on a card to take home, with the number on the back.
