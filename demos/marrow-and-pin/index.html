Stencil to skin

Every line starts on paper.

We draw it, print it as a stencil, lay it on you and look at it in the mirror together. Only then does the machine come out. Drag across a piece to put its stencil back over it, healed.

The studio

A private room, by appointment.

  1. Consultation, free, twenty minutesWith the artist you'd be booking. Bring photos, scribbles, the thing off your nan's teapot. We size it on your arm with a paper cut-out.
  2. A date and a £40 depositYou leave with a date and a price. The deposit comes off the price on the day.
  3. The dayThe drawing's ready, the stencil goes on, you check it in the mirror. Nobody watches unless you want them to.
House rules and aftercare
  • Over 18s. Photo ID every time, even if we tattooed you last month.
  • No drink, no drugs, and eat before you come in.
  • More than 15 minutes late and the deposit goes. We'll rebook you.
  • No hands, necks or faces on a first sitting.
  • Aftercare: film off after four hours in a warm shower, wash twice a day, a thin smear of balm, no swimming or sunbeds for two weeks. Ring us if it's hot or weeping.
