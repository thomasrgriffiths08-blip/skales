No. 03, extrait de parfum

Sel Obscur

Salt drawn off a black tide, and the smoke of a fire left burning on the shore.

Smell it hour by hour

Camille Auber walked the Guérande salt marsh at low tide and wanted the smell under the crust: wet granite, black water, and a driftwood fire further down the shore.

Sel Obscur is that walk, kept.

Mineral and smoke. Extrait de parfum at 24%, so it stays nine to eleven hours on skin. Composed by Camille Auber and filled by hand in Saint-Malo.

Eleven hours on skin

Drag through a day. The notes go out like a tide, one after another, and the salt stays to the end.

Fleur de sel

Guérande. The crust, and the first breath of it. With bergamot rind close behind.
