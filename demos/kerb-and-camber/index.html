The MOT price is capped by law

The DVSA sets a maximum fee for a class 4 MOT. Any garage charging you more than this is charging you more than it is allowed to.

One MOT, start to finish

While you wait

A car booked in at 08:30 is normally back on the road by 09:40. These are clock times off a job card, not an estimate.

08:30

Keys over the counter

Job card written: reg, mileage, and anything you have noticed — a noise, a light, a pull to one side.

08:45

On the ramp

Wheels off. Brakes, discs, hoses, suspension, steering, the whole underside, and the tester says what he sees as he goes.

09:05

Lights, tyres, glass

Headlamp aim on the beam setter, wipers, washers, horn, and tread depth measured on all four and written down in millimetres.

09:20

Emissions

Probe in the exhaust with the engine at temperature. Diesels get the smoke test, which is the noisy bit you can hear from the waiting room.

09:30

The result, in person

Pass, pass with advisories, or a fail — and if it is a fail you get shown the part, on the car, before anybody quotes you anything.

09:40

Away

Certificate on the DVSA system within minutes, next year's date texted to you, and the advisories written where you will find them again.

Tyres

The size is on the tyre wall

Three numbers on the side of the tyre, like 205/55 R16. Put them in and we will say whether it is on the rack now or coming tomorrow.

205/55 R16

In stock

Width is the tread in millimetres, profile is the sidewall as a percentage of that, and the rim is in inches. All three are moulded into the sidewall.

Every price is fitted: balanced, new valve, nuts torqued to the book, and the old tyre disposed of. We do not fit a tyre we would not put on our own car — if the size only comes in something we do not rate, we will say so on the phone.
