What the tester checks

About 45 minutes on the ramp. Your car stays in sight of the waiting room window.

  • Brakes, discs and brake lines
  • Steering and suspension
  • Tyres, measured on all four
  • Lights and headlamp aim
  • Exhaust and emissions
  • Seatbelts and airbag light
  • Body, chassis and corrosion
  • Wipers, washers, mirrors, horn

One MOT, start to finish

Clock times off a real job card from last Tuesday.

08:30

Keys over the counter

Reg, mileage and anything you have noticed: a noise, a light, a pull to one side.

08:45

On the ramp

Wheels off. Brakes, hoses, suspension, steering and the whole underside.

09:05

Lights, tyres, glass

Beam setter on the headlamps, tread depth written down in millimetres.

09:20

Emissions

Probe in the exhaust at temperature. Diesels get the smoke test.

09:30

The result, in person

Pass, pass with advisories or a fail, explained at the car.

09:40

Away

Certificate on the DVSA system, next year's date texted to you.

Tyres
