Cookies

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The little it stores is there because you are using it, like your place in a demo, and it stays in your browser.

What does the site store without asking?

Only what makes the pages you are using work. It stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

WhatWhereHow longWhy
Your answers on /start and your place in the demosSession storageUntil you close the tabSo the questions and the demos keep your answers as you move between screens.
Where you arrived from, such as the Instagram linkSession storageUntil you close the tabSo it can go with your booking, if you book a call.

Are there ad or analytics cookies?

No. The site runs no analytics or advertising tags at the moment. If that changes, a banner will ask you first, nothing will load unless you accept, and this page will list every cookie.

What about the booking calendar?

The booking page loads Calendly’s calendar so you can pick a time. Calendly sets its own cookies to make the booking work, under its own privacy policy.

How do you change your choice?

There is nothing to change at the moment. You can clear this site’s data in your browser settings at any time. The privacy notice covers everything else.
