The detailStraight answers

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes. It says so in its first sentence, on every call and every message. It sounds natural and it is polite, but nobody is tricked into thinking they are talking to a person, and anyone who would rather speak to you is told when you will ring them back.

What can it actually do on a call?

It offers only the times that are really free in your diary and books the job straight in. It quotes from your price list and says so when a job is not on it, rather than guessing. It asks the questions you always ask, texts a deposit link if you take deposits, and moves or cancels a booking when a customer asks.

What happens when it should not handle something?

It passes it to you. Anything urgent, unusual or outside your rules goes to your phone straight away with the caller’s details and what they need, and every call leaves you a short written summary, so you never ring someone back without knowing why.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

AI receptionist: questions people ask

Does it replace the person who answers my phone?

No. It covers the calls nobody can take: when you are on a job, with a customer or closed for the night. Anything it should not handle goes to a person with the details.

What if a caller asks something it cannot answer?

It says so, takes their details, tells them who will ring back, and sends you the note. It does not guess at prices or give advice it has not been given.

Can it use the number I already have?

Usually, yes. Calls you do not pick up are passed to it, so customers keep ringing the number they already know. We go through how it fits your phone on the call.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

The build is quoted flat after a short call, like everything else. Running it costs a little per minute of calls, billed by the phone and voice providers, and you get a straight figure for your call volume before anything starts.

Are the calls on the AI page real?

They are recorded examples for made-up businesses, and every voice in them is AI, the callers too. They show how a call goes and what the assistant does during it. Yours is set up on your own diary, price list and rules.
