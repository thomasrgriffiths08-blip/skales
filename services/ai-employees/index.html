The detailStraight answers

What jobs can an AI employee do?

The repetitive office work that follows every job: sending and chasing invoices, sorting the inbox and drafting replies, turning a voice note from the van into a quote, keeping the CRM up to date and booking the follow-ups. Each one is given a single job, the access it needs and the rules for doing it, the way you would brief a new starter.

Does it decide things on its own?

Only what you have said it can. Routine steps it does by itself and shows you afterwards. Anything that needs a judgement, such as a discount, a complaint or an unusual quote, it brings to you with the details and a suggested answer, and waits.

Where should I start?

With the job you put off most. For a lot of owners that is chasing money or the evening inbox. One AI employee doing one job well is worth more than a dozen half set up, and the next one can be added once the first has earned its place.

Straight answersThe questions people ask

AI employees: questions people ask

Is an AI employee just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions. An AI employee does a job: it reads the email, updates the record, sends the invoice and tells you what it did. It works in your tools rather than waiting in a chat window.

What if it makes a mistake?

Every AI employee keeps a log of what it did and why, and anything involving money or an unhappy customer can be set to wait for your yes. You check its work for the first few weeks, the same as you would with anyone new.

Will it replace my staff?

That is not the idea. It takes the repetitive admin off you and your team, so people spend their time on customers and the work rather than the paperwork.

How much does an AI employee cost?

Each one is quoted flat after a short call. The running cost is the AI and software it uses, which depends on how much work it does, and you get a straight figure before anything starts.

Are the numbers in the AI team demo real?

No. The AI team is a made-up building firm with illustrative numbers, there to show how the jobs are split and what each one gives back. Yours is set up on your own work.
