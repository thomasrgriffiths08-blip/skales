# Skales: search profile

This is the file every SEO, GEO and page-engine agent trains on. It holds who the business is, the only claims a page may make, the search each page should win, and what is off-limits. Hand edits by Tom override anything an agent infers. Agreed in the site plan on 8 October 2026.

## Identity
- **Full name:** Skales Studio. **Short form:** Skales. Use "Skales Studio" in schema, profiles and anywhere a search engine has to tell it apart from the SKALE crypto network.
- **Person:** Tom Griffiths (also Thomas Griffiths), founder and the only builder. Instagram @tomxsystems.
- **What it is:** a one-person UK studio that builds websites, online booking with a CRM and follow-up automation for UK service businesses, and runs Meta and Google ads that point at them.
- **Area:** United Kingdom. The town for the single location page is still to come from Tom. No other location pages, ever.
- **Domain:** skales.studio (bought 8 October 2026; the site runs on the github.io address until DNS is pointed).

## Facts bank (the only claims a page may make)
1. Builds websites, online booking and follow-up automation for UK service businesses (trades and local operators).
2. Runs Facebook and Instagram (Meta) ads, and Google search ads and Local Services Ads where the trade qualifies.
3. One person: the person you message is the person who builds it.
4. Thirty-six working demonstration builds run live on the site; every business in them is fictional, and each is labelled.
5. One system a week is built in public on Instagram.
6. Clients own their domain, code and accounts from day one; nothing is rented back to them monthly. Ad accounts are set up in the client's name.
7. Every build is hand-coded, with no page builder and no theme.
8. Every job is quoted flat after a short call and agreed before anything starts. No prices appear anywhere on the site.
9. A website on its own takes days; a full system with booking and follow-up usually takes one to two weeks.
10. The process: free teardown, flat quote, built in public, handover.
11. The live demos on /try/ run in the browser with a fictional business (Larchfield Heating in the films).

Anything not on this list, and not already on the site, does not get published. A page that needs a missing fact is blocked and the fact goes to Tom as a question.

## Voice and rules
- UK English. Plain, confident, short sentences, first person ("I build"). No exclamation marks, no "solutions", no "cutting-edge".
- No prices (ours or competitors'), no promised rankings or results, no invented numbers, clients, reviews or testimonials.
- No star ratings in schema until there are real reviews.
- Every page: one H1, two sentences under it that answer the page's question on their own, H2s written as the questions owners ask, one action at the end (book a call, or the 60-second check).
- Demos and films always carry "Fictional business · illustrative numbers".

## Do not touch
- The cold open on the home page and the 36 demo builds in /demos/.
- /start/ (noindex, built for the Instagram bio and ads).
- Tom's Instagram account: never visited, scraped or posted to.

## Competitor categories
- Pay-monthly trade website providers that lead with a low monthly price and shared templates.
- Standalone missed-call text-back apps.
- Page builders (Wix, Squarespace and similar).
- Directories and lead sites (Checkatrade, MyBuilder, Bark and similar).
None of them sells the website, booking, follow-up and ads as one system: that is the angle every page leads with. Named comparison is limited to page builders and directories, the names owners actually search.
