    04 · The details

    The parts you feel every day.

    The things that make bespoke joinery worth it are the ones you touch: the drawer that closes itself, the grain under your hand, the light at night.

    ▶ Watch it close
    Soft close

    Push it. It finishes the job.

    Handleless push-to-open fronts on full-extension runners. The damper catches the drawer in the last few centimetres and closes it silently.

    Veneer & solid oak

    Grain you can follow.

    Solid oak flutes and oak veneered panels, matched so the grain runs true across the wall.

    Light, built in

    Glow, no glare.

    Warm LED set into the underside of each shelf, wired in during the build so no cable ever shows.
