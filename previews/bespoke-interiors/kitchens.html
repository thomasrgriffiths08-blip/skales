Questions

Good to know.

How much does a bespoke kitchen cost?

Every kitchen is made for one room, so the honest answer comes after Mark has measured up. The guide prices in the designer above give you a starting point. Your fixed price comes with your drawings, with no surprises after.

Do you make the kitchens yourselves?

Yes. Doors, carcasses and islands are built in our workshop in Maynooth and fitted by our own team, so the people who make it are the people who fit it.

Can you work with my appliances and worktop choice?

Yes. We plan around integrated or freestanding appliances, and can supply quartz, solid oak, stone and other worktops.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the kitchen and your finishes. Mark gives you a clear timeline at the design visit, and fitting is planned to keep your kitchen out of action for as little time as possible.

Do you cover my area?

We work across Dublin and Kildare and the surrounding counties. Book a design visit and we will confirm.
