Questions

Good to know.

Can you fit wardrobes under a sloped ceiling?

Yes. Sloped ceilings, chimney breasts and awkward alcoves are exactly what made-to-measure is for. Every wardrobe is built to your room and scribed on site.

Hinged or sliding doors?

Hinged doors give full access to every section. Sliding doors suit tighter rooms. Mark will recommend what works best for your space at the design visit.

What can go inside?

Shelving, drawers, double hanging rails, shoe storage, pull-out trouser rails, jewellery drawers and LED lighting. The inside is planned around what you own.

How long does fitting take?

Most bedrooms are fitted in a day or two once made. You get a clear timeline at the design visit.
