Questions

Good to know.

What height should wainscoting be?

A classic dado height is around 900mm. Higher ceilings suit a centre rail or taller panels. Mark will suggest the right proportions for your room.

Which rooms suit panelling?

Living rooms, hallways, bedrooms, stairs and dining rooms all work well, and it adds character to newer homes.

Do you paint it too?

We can supply it primed or fully finished in your chosen colour.

How long does it take?

Most single walls or rooms are fitted in a day or two. You get a clear timeline at the design visit.
