    Questions

    Good to know.

    Can you hide all the cables?

    Yes. Sockets, HDMI and power are planned into the drawing so the TV floats on the wall with nothing hanging down.

    Can you include an electric fire?

    Yes. Media walls can include an inset electric fire, a soundbar shelf and closed storage for consoles and boxes.

    What finishes are available?

    Solid oak, oak veneer, fluted panels and spray-painted finishes in any colour. Mark brings samples to the design visit.

    How long does fitting take?

    Most media walls are fitted in a couple of days once made in the workshop. You get a clear timeline at the design visit.
