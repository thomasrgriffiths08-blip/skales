Questions

Good to know.

Do you handle the whole bathroom?

Yes. Design, supply, plumbing, electrics, tiling and carpentry are all handled by our team, so you have one point of contact.

Can you do wet rooms and walk-in showers?

Yes, along with family bathrooms, en-suites and spa-style rooms. Mark will talk through what works in your space.

Will I know the price up front?

Yes. You get clear pricing with no hidden costs before any work starts.
