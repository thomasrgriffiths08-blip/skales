Questions

Good to know.

What if my walls are not straight?

They rarely are. Every unit is scribed on site to your walls and ceiling so there are no gaps.

Can you build in a TV or speakers?

Yes. TV units, speaker spaces and hidden cabling can all be planned into your alcoves.

Do you paint them?

Yes. Units are finished in your chosen colour, ready to use.

Who will I deal with?

Mark, the owner. He measures, designs and oversees the fit himself.
